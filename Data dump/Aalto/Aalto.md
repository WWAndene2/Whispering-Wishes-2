## Review

**Ratings**: Hybrid — T4 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: playable as both Main DPS and buffer; good overall buffing potential; relatively short
support rotation; only Aero damage buffer in the game; clones taunt enemies, improving endgame
survivability; cool sunglasses.

**Cons**: slightly dependent on S1 for smoother support rotations; some Inherent Skills do essentially
nothing; needs to shoot exclusively through his gates for extra damage, troublesome vs moving targets;
literally no AoE potential; underwhelming Liberation at S0 outside Concerto generation; no current meta
place, outshined in his best supporting slot.

**Summary**: a unique character, also playable as a Hybrid buffer via misty gates that empower attacks
shot through them; can turn incorporeal to increase movement speed and spawn taunting mist clones. His
Outro buffs Aero DPS, but as a Hybrid he's outshined in Jiyan teams by Mortefi (stronger, on every
Jiyan banner, heavy-attack-focused synergy). As Main DPS, invested seriously, he can fully clear Tower
endgame if mastered — his main DPS potential comes from mid-air attacks (8-10 pistol shots, buffed
through his Ultimate's gates), but falls short of the best in his role both damage- and Energy-wise
(150 Energy cost on his Ultimate is tricky to sustain). Difficult to play/learn due to needing to shoot
through specific areas and time jumps. Summary: subpar but viable DPS and Hybrid option, outclassed but
usable if played well.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Rotation** (fastest, most consistent Concerto rotation — no Forte weaving by default): Skill: Mist
Avatar (during another character's rotation, to let it cool down) → Intro → Liberation → Skill: Mist
Avatar (if off cooldown) → Basic P1-5 → Echo → Outro. Firing through Mist Gates isn't required to hit
the Concerto threshold for his Outro in this rotation.

**Synergies**: Ciaccona (best partner for Main DPS Aalto, core mono-Aero team member, enables Windward
Pilgrimage). Jiyan (not optimal, but a viable replacement via his 23% Aero DMG Amp Outro). Iuno (same
— viable replacement, not optimal). Rover: Aero (core mono-Aero partner, amplifies Aero DMG team-wide
and raises max Aero Erosion stacks). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing Supports).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (DPS), Best Team (Hybrid), F2P Team (DPS) — exact roster icons weren't
machine-readable in this extraction; team names are directly from the source text.
