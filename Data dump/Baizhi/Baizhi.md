## Review

**Ratings**: Support — T3 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T3 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: incredible supportive Outro (all-type DMG Amp that isn't lost on switch); good healing for
survivability, especially with Sequences (S6 can also buff Glacio DMG); very universal buffs, slots
into most teams; effectively free; S5 grants a cheat-death full revive.

**Cons**: direct competition with the superior Shorekeeper (bigger buffs) and Verina (shorter rotation);
many of her buffs aren't compatible with Coordinated Attackers; picking up Euphonia can make rotations
awkward; rotations can run long, sometimes forcing dodges; most attacks have a slight delay since her
pet needs to attack for her.

**Summary**: a 4★ free Glacio Rectifier Support providing wide-ranging buffs and healing utility.
Buffs apply to only one team member at a time, making her incompatible with Coordinated Attackers. Her
Forte gauge (4 stacks, from Basic Attacks) is depleted via Skill or Heavy Attack, generating Concerto
Energy and healing every 2s. Skill pulses Glacio DMG while healing the team; Liberation heals and
launches Remnant Entities (a Coordinated Attack). Inherent Skill 1's Euphonia (15% ATK for 20s) should
be picked up by the Main DPS — recommended to swap to them right after casting Skill. Meta position:
awkward — incompatible with Jinhsi teams (which typically run two Coordinated Attackers), slower
rotation than Verina, less potent buffs than Shorekeeper. Ultimately falls short of the 5★ generalists
who do everything she does, better — but remains a good free option any team can run.

## Build

**Best Weapons**: Stellar Symphony (R1, Shorekeeper's signature) 100.00% — best overall. Variation
(R5) — utility-focused, prioritizes Concerto rotation speed. Call of the Abyss (R5) — best F2P option
(free via Rinascita Exploration Quests). Rectifier of Voyager (R5) — accessible option with a flat
Resonance Energy proc. Rectifier#25 (R5) — decent F2P craftable option.

**Best Echo Sets**: #1 Rejuvenating Glow, main echo Fallacy of No Return (a strong option for maximizing
Energy Regen / more permanent party buffing) — Bell-Borne Geochelone is a worse but acceptable
alternative if a good Fallacy isn't available (shorter buff uptime, only 10% DMG Bonus/15s, but blocks
50% DMG from up to 3 hits). Special/situational: Moonlit Clouds, main echo Impermanence Heron.

**Substats priority**: Energy Regen (until satisfied) > HP% > Flat HP. Crit Rate/Crit DMG are fine if
minor extra personal damage is desired (negligible).

**Endgame stat targets (Lv.90)**: HP 20000-35000+, DEF 1100+, Crit Rate 60%+, Crit DMG 210%+, Energy
Regen 220-230% (by far the most important stat — her personal damage investment is fully skippable),
Glacio DMG Bonus 0-30%.

**Skill priority**: Resonance Liberation > Resonance Skill > Forte Circuit > Intro Skill > Basic
Attack.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Rotation S0R0** [6.50s regular / 5.50s at R1]: Intro → Basic P1-4 → Skill (swap, collect Euphonia
with your DPS) → Liberation (delayed to cancel her slow swap-in attack) → Basic P1-3 → Echo → Outro.

**Rotation S0R3** [4.31s]: Intro → Liberation → Basic P1-4 → Echo → Skill → Outro.

A Concerto weapon cuts down the final Basic count and is best equipped on Baizhi for overall team DPS
(minimizing her field time). Use Skill whenever 4 Concentration stacks are built, Heavy Attack
otherwise.

**Synergies**: very flexible, slots into most teams as the Support. Incompatible with Coordinated
Attack Hybrids (Yinlin, Zhezhi, Mortefi) since she can only buff one character at a time via her Outro
and Inherent Skill 1.

**Example Teams**: none specifically named — the source states she's "very flexible and can be used in
any team in the Support slot."
