## Review

- DPS Tier: T1.5 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Hybrid Tier: T1.5 / T2. Value Tier
  (DPS): T3 / T4. Value Tier (Hybrid): T2 / T2.
- **Pros**: strong Fusion DMG + Skill DMG Amp Outro (pairs with many DPS); works as both Solo DPS and
  multi-DPS unit thanks to real personal damage; almost entirely airborne (dodges most enemy attacks
  free); free team Sustain (healing + strong shielding); very simple to play (mostly hold Basic
  Attack).
- **Cons**: signature weapon is key to his full potential (his dedicated Echo set is hard to use
  without it); high stat/gear requirements (Energy Regen + Crit both needed — one of the hardest
  characters to build); low interruption resistance on key attacks (especially his Forte Skill),
  often needing Swap-Cancels or Perfect Dodges.
- Fusion Sword Hybrid: strong personal damage + can buff another DPS via Outro, enabling multi-DPS team
  archetypes instead of single-target damage concentration.
- Forte "Bravo" (cap 100) → Returned from Ashes: huge damage, fills half his Concerto, scales off Basic
  Attack DMG. Filled via Basic Attacks (grounded gives little; Mid-air Basic Attacks are the main
  source), Intro Skill, and (rarely worth it) regular Resonance Skill hits.
  Holding Basic Attack triggers automatic backflips (extra damage) and Charged Attacks, the efficient
  way to fill Bravo.
- Intro: minor damage + generates 1/4 Bravo + starts Mid-air sequence at Stage 2 instead of 1 — makes
  Intro essential to optimized rotations.
- Ultimate: minor AoE damage + heals the party (scales with ER) + boosts the flat-ATK-from-ER bonus
  (Theatrical Moment → "My" Moment) + instantly airborne + doubles Bravo generation from all non-Intro
  sources.
- Gameplay loop: Intro → Ultimate → hold Basic Attack until Bravo is full → Forte cast → swap out. His
  Forte hits hard enough to justify a traditional Hypercarry team build (though that underuses his
  Outro).
- Build requirement: massive Energy Regen need — flat ATK scales with ER over 150% (cap 280% incl.
  base 100%), required for Ultimate access every rotation, and his signature Echo set (Tidebreaking
  Courage) needs 250% total ER. ~160-170% ER (incl. base) is enough to guarantee Ultimate uptime if you
  drop to Molten Rift 5pc instead. Signature Weapon (Unflickering Valor) solves most of his Energy
  Regen needs while also boosting Basic ATK DMG and Crit Rate — "reaches a different level of viability
  when built properly."
- Meta position: doesn't replace a generalist healer (Shorekeeper/Verina) since he wants to deal damage
  too, but stacks well alongside one. Outro's Resonance Skill + Fusion DMG Amp combo is valued by
  Changli/Chixia (Fusion) and Carlotta/Jinhsi/general Skill-DMG dealers. Strong in Mono Fusion with
  Lupa. Without his Signature, value becomes questionable vs. running Lupa+Encore+generalist Support
  instead.

## Build

**Best Weapon**: Unflickering Valor (R1, signature) — Crit Rate +8%; Liberation cast → +24% Basic
Attack DMG Bonus for 10s; dealing Basic Attack DMG → +24% Basic Attack DMG Bonus for 4s. 100%. Alts:
Laser Shearer 77.2%, Bloodpact's Pledge 75.1%, Red Spring 73.1% (Camellya's signature, usable on
Basic-ATK Sword characters), Emerald of Genesis 71.3% (best F2P-craftable, only works with Tidebreaking
Courage at R5).

**Best Echo Set**: Tidebreaking Courage — 2pc: ER +10%. 5pc: ATK +15%; at 250%+ ER, all-Attribute DMG
+30%. Requires very high investment (signature weapon or excellent Echoes) but is his best damage
option when reachable. Fallback: Molten Rift 5pc (2pc: +10% Fusion DMG; 5pc: Skill cast → +30% Fusion
DMG for 15s) if 250% ER is out of reach — cast a Skill and cancel it via Ultimate to trigger this at
rotation start. Main Echo: Dragon of Dirge (+12% Basic ATK + 12% Fusion DMG Bonus in main slot; best
used pre-rotation since its Transform cast is slow).

**Substats priority**: Energy Regen (until 250%) > Crit Rate = Crit DMG > Energy Regen (above 250%) >
Basic Attack DMG% > ATK% > ATK. ER/Fusion DMG main stats on 3-cost pieces only realistically achievable
via his signature (ER surpassing 280%); otherwise always prioritize ER/ER.

**Endgame stat targets (Lv.90)**: HP 16000+, DEF 1300+, ATK 1600-2000+ (before Forte buffs), Crit Rate
65-80%+, Crit DMG 210-260%+, Energy Regen 250-280%, Fusion DMG Bonus 10-40%.

**Skill priority**: Forte Circuit > Basic Attack > Liberation > Intro > Skill.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Standard Rotation**: Intro → Skill: Plunging Attack (optional, immediately cancelled with Ultimate —
used to trigger Resonance-Skill-conditional Weapon/Echo effects without losing time) → Ultimate →
Mid-Air Atk P2 → P2: Charged Attack → P2: Flip → P3 → P3: Flip → Forte: Returned from Ashes → Outro.

**Double Forte DPS Rotation** (Main DPS variant, lower DPS but higher total damage — only worth it if
heavily buffed): Skill: Anchors Aweigh! (pre-rotation) → swap → Intro → Mid-Air Atk P2 → Charged Attack
→ Flip → P3 → Flip → P4 → swap + prepare team buffs → Intro → Forte: Returned from Ashes → Ultimate →
Mid-Air Atk P1 → P2 → Charged Attack → Flip → P3 → Flip → P4 → Forte: Returned from Ashes → Outro.

**Synergies**: Lupa (his best buffer — cornerstone of Mono Fusion, Outro gives Basic + Fusion DMG Amp —
"top tier pair"). Fusion DPS partners for a Brant+Lupa duo: Galbrena/Aemeath/Changli/Encore/Chixia —
Changli has the technically-best synergy (leverages his Outro most), but Galbrena/Aemeath can outperform
via higher baseline damage. Skill-DMG Main DPS alternatives (leveraging his Outro's Skill DMG Amp):
Carlotta, Jinhsi. Generalist supports: Mornye (best with Lupa — enables Hypercarry while keeping Lupa's
Mono Fusion buffs), Shorekeeper (strong CRIT buffs), Verina (most accessible fallback).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, Mono Fusion Alternatives, More Alternative Teams (with Jinhsi, Verina
required for Coordinated Attacks), DPS Brant (Mornye best only with Lupa present) — exact roster icons
weren't machine-readable in this extraction; inferred combinations from the Synergies section text.
