### Active skills — Hexagram Calls, Lightning Falls

**Basic Attack**: up to 4 attacks, Electro DMG.
**Heavy Attack — Mountain Over Thunder**: hold Basic Attack, consumes the highlighted Trigram-Mountain
then Trigram-Thunder (left to right), Electro DMG.
**Heavy Attack — Thunder Over Mountain**: consumes Trigram-Thunder then Trigram-Mountain, small Electro
DMG, also reduces the target's Vibration Strength.
**Heavy Attack — Twin Mountains**: consumes 2 Trigram-Mountain, heals all nearby team Resonators
(no direct damage).
**Heavy Attack — Twin Thunders**: consumes 2 Trigram-Thunder, heals all nearby team Resonators once per

Basic Attack Stage 2 hit. Trigram-Thunder gained on Basic Attack Stage 4 or Mid-air Attack hit, or on

## Review

- DPS Tier (Support): T2 (Tower of Adversity), T3 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: same, T2/T3.
- **Pros**: good generalist buffing reminiscent of Verina/Baizhi, with off-field healing AND Electro
  Flare application (possibly relevant for future synergies); needs close to no investment to perform;
  a 4★ character, easy to access permanently alongside Sequence nodes; basically always better than
  Baizhi — a rare 4★ release with a genuinely solid use case.
- **Cons**: almost always worse than Verina as a pure generalist — endgame accounts that already own
  specialized Supports (various Rover attunements, Chisa, Lupa) plus Shorekeeper may never field her;
  a lot of her buffing potential is tied to Skill-damage dealers, narrowing which teams she stands out
  in, and her S6 (the biggest piece of that value) isn't easily reached short-term; lacks a Death Resist
  ability like Baizhi's S5 or Shorekeeper/Verina's 2nd passives.
- 4★ Electro Rectifier Support with healing, Electro Flare/Rage application, and general + specialized
  (Skill-damage-focused) buffs. Easy to play with genuinely viable buffs, particularly for Skill DMG
  Main DPS like Carlotta and Phrolova.
- Key buffs when fully rotating: 10% team Skill DMG Bonus for 24s after casting her Enhanced Ultimate
  AND any ally's Intro (up to 25% with a 2nd Intro cast during that window, 50% at S6); 15% team DMG
  Amp for 30s on Outro (doesn't expire on swap); 25% team ATK for 20s with Rejuvenating Glow +
  Fallacy of No Return (her recommended default Echo loadout); healing on Intro, some Heavy Attacks,
  and Outro (constant off-field healing on the active character); periodic Electro Flare (→ Electro
  Rage at 10+ stacks) via Intro and enhanced Ultimate; minor enemy grouping via Skill.
- Most of her kit builds toward the Enhanced Ultimate — activated via 2 of her unique Heavy Attacks,
  requiring at least 4 Trigrams generated/consumed to reach it and apply most of her buffs before ending
  the rotation.
- Moveset/rotation: generates Thunder Trigrams (Basic 4, Skill, Mid-air Attack) far more easily than
  Mountain Trigrams (only Basic 2), so in practice "Mountain over Thunder" + "Twin Thunders" are the
  fastest-reachable Heavy Attack combo. Dodge Counter transitions directly into Basic Attack 3 and can
  be freely used mid-rotation; even with zero Mountain Trigrams generated, all Heavy Attacks grant equal
  Concerto Energy so the rotation still works.
- Meta position: a solid universal Support that outperforms Baizhi (more personal damage, more
  consistent rotation, similar general buffs, stronger specialized ones) but doesn't outclass dedicated
  or generalist 5★ Supports like Shorekeeper/Verina — Verina's very short rotation matches most of her
  buffing strength already, making Buling's slightly higher personal damage insufficient to stand out,
  even at S6. She does match Verina specifically in Carlotta/Phrolova teams (both Skill-DMG-focused),
  buffing them at a similar level with a slightly longer rotation (only relevant at S6). Sees almost no
  use with Coordinated-Attack-dependent Skill DPS like Jinhsi, since she lacks Coordinated Attacks
  herself. A viable alternative Support choice, especially in Skill-damage-focused teams, but will lack
  a spot on many optimized endgame accounts.

## Build

**Best Weapons** (ranked, no % shown in this source): 1. Stringmaster (R1) — the best overall weapon
for all Rectifier damage dealers; even as a mostly-non-damage Support, none of her buffs scale off a
particular stat/condition, so optimizing her own damage is still worthwhile, and Stringmaster's huge
universal damage buffs make it her strongest option too. 2. Lethean Elegy (R1, Phrolova's signature) —
another strong generalist option, less punchy than Stringmaster but her 2nd best. 3. Rime-Draped
Sprouts (R1) — mostly an ATK/Crit DMG stat-stick, she can barely stack its Basic-Attack-DMG passive but
it's still decently usable. 4. Luminous Hymn (R1) — simple ATK/Crit Rate stat-stick (Spectro Frazzle
passive unusable). 5. Cosmic Ripples (R1, listed as her **strongest permanently available option**) —
ER+12.8%, decent Basic Attack DMG stacking she uses reasonably well; one of the strongest ways to boost
her Healing capability while also adding solid damage, worth considering even over the higher-ranked
options above to optimize her Sustain specifically. 6. Waltz in Masquerade (R5) — fully F2P-accessible
craftable 4★ that capitalizes on her instant Electro Flare application to quickly max its ATK-stacking
passive; still pales vs. any 5★ due to low base ATK.

**Best Echo Sets**: Rejuvenating Glow (only set listed) — 2pc: Healing+10%; 5pc: healing an ally → team
ATK+15% for 30s (easy to keep active 100% of the time, triggerable via character abilities or weapon
effects). Main Echo options: Fallacy of No Return (preferred — 10% ER + team-wide 10% ATK on use for
20s, favored for max energy generation or more-permanent party buffing vs. Bell-Borne Geochelone) or
Bell-Borne Geochelone (a worse DMG option nowadays due to shorter buff uptime — only +10% DMG Bonus for
15s — but still good if a strong Fallacy roll isn't available; also blocks 50% DMG from up to 3 hits).

**Substats priority**: Energy Regen (until satisfied) > Crit Rate = Crit DMG > ATK% > ATK. An Energy
Regen 3-cost piece may be necessary before S2 without good substats; ER requirements drop significantly
from S2 onward.

**Endgame stat targets (Lv.90)**: HP 15000+, DEF 1150+, ATK 1500-2000+, Crit Rate 60-80%+, Crit DMG
215-280%+, Energy Regen 125-180% (125-135% at S2 in Carlotta [lower]/Phrolova [higher] teams, 160-180%
in the same teams before S2), Electro DMG Bonus 0-60%+.

**Sequence review** (personal-damage-focused Sequence % isn't meaningful for a Support — notes instead):
S1 low value (+20% Crit Rate on her strongest attack, small personal DPS boost, not worth chasing). S2
medium value (large Energy refund lowers ER requirements significantly, potentially freeing both cost-3
Echo pieces for damage stats instead). S3-S4 low value (minor sustain-only increases, situational,
zero team-damage contribution). S5 low value (extra Electro Flare application, minor personal-damage
increase, could matter more with future Electro Flare synergies). S6 HIGH value — her single most
important node, upgrading the team's Skill DMG Bonus from 25% to 50% for the Main DPS, a real
buffing-capability leap that differentiates her from Verina in Skill-DMG-oriented teams.

**Skill priority**: Forte Circuit > Basic Attack > Resonance Skill > Intro Skill > Resonance Liberation
(Skill/Intro/Liberation can all be skipped when leveling to save resources at minimal damage loss).

## Gameplay and Teams

A simple, linear Support rotation — little room for optimization beyond learning it correctly, lacking
strong Swap Cancel windows. Generates her 4 required Trigrams via Basic 2, Basic 4, Skill, and Mid-air
Attack. **Echo timing**: best used right before Outro to maximize buff uptime.

**Opener Rotation** (used when she's opening the team's rotation cycle without an Intro, as is typical
for her as the team's Support): Basic 1 → Basic 2 → Basic 3 → Basic 4 (Skill-cancel endlag) → Skill →
Basic 4 → Heavy: Twin Thunders → Heavy: Mountain Over Thunder (Ultimate-cancel endlag) → Ultimate:
Flashing Thunder Spell - Harmony → Outro.

**Loop Rotation** (when an Intro is available — grants extra Concerto Energy, shorter than doing 2
Basic 4's): Intro → Basic 1 → Basic 2 (Jump-cancel endlag) → Jump → Basic: Mid-air Attack (Skill-cancel
endlag) → Skill → Basic 4 → Heavy: Mountain Over Thunder → Heavy: Twin Thunders → Ultimate: Flashing
Thunder Spell - Harmony → Outro.

**Synergies**: Carlotta/Phrolova — her best DPS characters to buff, both being Main Skill-DMG-oriented
DPS. Via her Outro, Echo Set, and passive (enhanced at S6), she provides them a wide array of ATK, DMG
Amplification, and Skill DMG Bonus buffs, making her a solid Support choice for those teams particularly
once S6 is unlocked.

**Example Teams**: Team #1: Best Team, Team #2: F2P Team — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable
in this extraction; team names/conditions are directly from the source text.

**Real bugs found and fixed (2026-09-03)**:
1. `bestEchoes` was `['Fallacy of No Return', 'Bell-Borne Geochelone', 'Rejuvenating Glow 5pc']` — both
   `Fallacy of No Return` and `Bell-Borne Geochelone` are alternative Main Echoes under the SAME single
   set (Rejuvenating Glow), but `getSonataLoadouts()` pairs entries sequentially as `[main, set]` —
   with only one trailing set string for two main-echo strings, the parser paired the 2nd main echo
   with the set and left the 1st (the source's actually-preferred pick) as an orphaned row with no set
   attached at all. Fixed by repeating the set name for each alt main echo:
   `['Fallacy of No Return', 'Rejuvenating Glow 5pc', 'Bell-Borne Geochelone', 'Rejuvenating Glow 5pc']`
   — the same fix pattern as Sanhua's reversed-order `bestEchoes` bug fixed earlier this pass.
2. `buling.blocks.js`'s `buling.heavy.twin-thunders` block modeled Heavy Attack - Twin Thunders as a
   real `'damage'`-kind block (category `basicDmg`, 169 flat + 18.30% ATK). This source's own
   Multipliers table explicitly labels that exact value **"Healing"**, not damage — Twin Thunders is a
   pure team-heal move (once/s for 8s), dealing zero direct damage, the same as Twin Mountains (which
   already correctly has no damage block at all). The old block was silently crediting the character's
   computed DPS with a healing formula. Removed the block entirely — the rotation step now correctly
   resolves to 0 direct damage, same treatment as her Outro (an intentional zero-DMG utility step).
3. The DPS-tier table entry was `['Buling', 'T1.5', 'T2']` (ToA/WW) — this source's Ratings section
   AND its Value Tier List both agree on T2 (ToA) / T3 (WW). Fixed to `['Buling', 'T2', 'T3']`.

**Full re-audit (2026-09-08, explicitly requested — including cross-interactions and legacy-vs-modern
engine logic, not just data values).** Traced her S6 chain fix all the way through the legacy engine's
own `applyResonanceChain()` (calcEngine.js) instead of trusting the modern engine block's own comment

previously-undocumented completeness gaps. Nothing here duplicates the two prior passes' own findings.

1. **The 2026-09-04 chain.s6 fix comment mischaracterized the legacy engine's real gap.** That comment
   claimed the legacy RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA/CHAR_BUFF_TABLE path "still stores the absolute 50," implying
   a double-counting risk (75% total) mirroring the bug already fixed in the modern engine. Traced
   `applyResonanceChain()` line by line: its non-main-DPS branch (the one that runs for Buling in her
   real, near-universal role as a Support, since she's essentially never the team's main DPS) never
   reads `skillDmg` at all for a teammate — and `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE['Buling'].libBuffs` (the actual,
   correctly-gated mechanism that delivers a support's team buff to a real DPS) is a flat,
   sequence-unaware 25% with no S6-conditional entry anywhere. So `RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA['Buling'].s6`'s
   `skillDmg:50` is genuinely UNREACHABLE in the legacy engine for her actual use case — the real bug is
   total OMISSION (the team stays stuck at 25% even at S6), not double-counting. This is a real,
   user-facing gap: the legacy "RAW" DPS tier is directly surfaced (TeamsTab.jsx's share-stats line,
   DPSComparisonCard.jsx), so an S6 Buling's real, documented buff silently under-delivers there. Not
   fixed in this pass — there's no existing schema precedent anywhere in the roster for a
   sequence-conditional `libBuffs` value, so building one would be a roster-wide schema change, out of
   scope for a single-character audit without explicit sign-off — but the misleading comment (in both
   `buling.blocks.js` and `RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA['Buling']`'s own entry) is corrected to accurately
   describe the real failure mode, and flagged clearly for a future decision.
2. **Resonance Skill's "Pull-in Effect" continuous DMG (5.84%×10) had no block at all**, despite being
   a real, sourced SKILL_MULTIPLIERS row for the SAME cast as Thunder Talisman (one kit-text sentence —
