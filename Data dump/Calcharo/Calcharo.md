## Review

**Ratings**: DPS — T4 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA Value),
T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: good damage ceiling when played perfectly (especially his hardest rotation, competitive with
high tiers); more consistent with swap cancelling mastery.

**Cons**: the most inconsistent character in the game, suffers most from interruption/enemy
movement/dodging; mistakes cost a lot of damage; hardest rotation to execute in the game; realistically
close to no AoE; swap cancelling trades damage for consistency; only realistically works with Yinlin
(a limited 5★); his Intro Skill has a long wind-up costing time on Tower of Adversity runs.

**Summary**: the hardest character to play at an optimized level. An Electro Broadblade 5★ Main DPS
from the standard banner, shining brightest in Hypercarry teams via his Ultimate-state bursts. Either
good or really bad — no in-between, and not recommended to the average casual player. His Death
Messengers (activated via 5 Basic Attacks then a Heavy Attack in Ultimate state) are the bulk of his
damage, but their long wind-ups are easily interrupted; swap-cancelling avoids that but forfeits Outro
buffs from his hybrid partner (usually Yinlin). Realistically capped at 2-3 Death Messengers per
rotation rather than the theoretical maximum, and even then doesn't surpass an optimized Encore.
Revealed in beta to be underwhelming at a casual level, at best mid-high tier damage played perfectly —
not recommended unless you like the character or want the challenge.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Warm-Up** (2 options, before the burst combo): Option 1 (higher Concerto/Energy gain and more weapon
bonus uptime, riskier) — Basic P1 → Skill P1 → Basic P2 → Skill P2 → Basic P3 → Basic P4 → Skill P3
(cancels weak part of Basic P4) → Heavy: Mercy (swap-cancel if possible). Option 2 (faster, safer,
dodge-compatible) — Skill P1 → P2 → P3 → Heavy: Mercy (swap-cancel if possible).

**Optimized Burst Combo** (Dash Cancel-based, targets 3 realistic Death Messengers): Echo (swap cancel)
→ Intro → Liberation → Heavy: Death Messenger (optional swap cancel) → Basic: Hounds Roar P1 → P2
(Dash Cancel) → P1 → P2 (Dash Cancel) → P1 → Heavy: Death Messenger (optional swap cancel) → [repeat]
→ Heavy: Death Messenger (optional swap cancel) → Outro.

**Basic Burst Combo** (spam-clickable, slower, less effective): same skeleton without Dash Cancels,
Hounds Roar P1-5 in full each cycle.

**4 Death Messenger Combo** (Difficulty: Impossible): the same Dash Cancel structure repeated a 4th
time — theoretically possible, "widely considered one of the hardest rotations in the game."

**Synergies**: Lynae (best overall buffer — 40% DMG Amp to his main damage types, 15% general/25%
Liberation — best choice in Hypercarry compositions). Yinlin (most reliable Outro buffer, 25%
Liberation/20% Electro, also strong off-field Coordinated Attack damage; swap-cancel compatible).
Mornye/Shorekeeper/Verina (the 3 best generalist Supports — Mornye has special synergy with Lynae via
Tune Rupture/Strain response, best choice when Lynae is present; Shorekeeper close second via massive
Crit buffs; Verina most accessible).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Lynae + Mornye — Mornye is only best with Lynae present), Budget Team
(entirely incompatible with Swap Cancels) — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this
extraction; team names/notes are directly from the source text.
