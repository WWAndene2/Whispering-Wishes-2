## Review

- DPS Tier (Hybrid role): T2 (Tower of Adversity), T3 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T3 (ToA
  Value), T3 (WW Value).
- **Pros**: super accessible weapon-wise — signature makes a negligible difference over the always-
  available Emerald of Genesis; extremely quickswap-friendly and consistent when optimized; works well
  with both mid-air and ground-based teammates; high damage for her rotation time with a nice Outro buff.
- **Cons**: needs significant optimization (Animation/Swap Cancels) to be worth playing at all — no
  other justification for fielding her; very reliant on a limited teammate pool for real performance
  gains (especially Lupa); lower damage ceiling than more modern Fusion options — little reason to pull
  her fresh if you don't already own her.
- 5★ Fusion Sword Hybrid-DPS built around mid-air combat, short-rotation-when-played-correctly Mono
  Fusion archetype character; unique 10s-long Outro buff (tied with Lumi for the shortest in the game)
  that defines a lot of her synergies; unusually complex to optimize — took a long time post-release to
  be fully figured out.
- Key mechanic: Enflamement (Forte stacks, cap 4) is her whole kit's foundation, tied to True Sight.
  Gained via Ultimate (instant full 4 stacks) or True Sight attacks (1 each). At 4 stacks, Forte Heavy
  Attack (Flaming Sacrifice) fires — big damage + large Resonance/Concerto Energy refund. Rotation goal:
  4 different True Sight attacks → Forte Heavy → Ultimate → 2nd Forte Heavy → Outro-swap (2 Forte
  Heavies per rotation). The post-Ultimate Forte Heavy gets +25% ATK (Fiery Feather); via Inherent
  Skills, True Sight attacks scale +5% Fusion DMG per Enflamement stack held, and Forte Heavies/
  Ultimate get +20% Fusion DMG + 15% DEF Ignore.
- True Sight: opened by Intro, Skill, or Basic/Mid-air Attack Stage 4, lasts 12s — during it, a single
  special Basic Attack fires (Conquest on ground, Charge mid-air), each granting 1 Enflamement on hit.
  Skill (2 charges, 12s CD) is her main Enflamement engine alongside Intro and a 2nd Intro/3rd Skill for
  the last stack. Nearly all her key animations (Basic 4, True Sight attacks, Skill, Forte Heavies) are
  long and interruption-prone — Swap Cancelling is her only full protection, and the foundation of her
  optimal (quickswap) playstyle.
- Meta position: entirely defined by Lupa's existence and Quickswap. Lupa's Mono Fusion buffs make her
  an ideal Outro-buff recipient and buffer for Changli; Brant offers a similarly ideal Outro pairing
  with strong personal damage. Without the Quickswap edge, Changli is outclassed by more modern Fusion
  DPS dealing comparable damage with better buffs and lower field time (Brant, and especially Lupa).
  Performance is heavily player-dependent — shines for experts, struggles for casuals in the current meta.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Opener** (2-part combo, executed at battle start when Ultimate is immediately available — free
damage, recommended before any other character's rotation since Ultimate typically recovers by the
time 4 Enflamement stacks are reached again): Ultimate → Forte: Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice (swap).

**Standard Rotation** (generates full Forte stacks with a single Intro and no swaps — fastest possible
Enflamement generation for that constraint; ideal for keeping Outro buffs constantly active in trios
like Changli+Brant+Lupa): Intro → Basic: True Sight: Charge → Skill → Heavy Attack → Basic: True Sight:
Charge → Basic: Mid-air Attack (Instant Dash Cancel) → Dash → Basic: Mid-air Attack 4 → Basic: True
Sight: Charge → Skill → Basic: True Sight: Conquest → Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice → Ultimate → Heavy:
Flaming Sacrifice (Swap) → Outro. (Letting the last Heavy occur naturally without swapping keeps Outro
buffs active and allows an Echo cast — extends the rotation by 1.37s.)

**Double Intro Approach** (costs a teammate their Intro entirely — strong if that teammate's kit
doesn't need it, lets Changli skip Basic Attacks for Enflamement entirely): Intro → True Sight: Charge
→ Skill → True Sight: Conquest (swap) → (build 2nd character's Intro) → Intro → True Sight: Charge →
Skill → True Sight: Conquest → Skill → True Sight: Conquest → Forte: Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice →
Ultimate → Forte: Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice (swap) → Outro.

**Swap Cancels**: her 4 key Swap-Cancellable abilities are Basic P4, Skill (True Sight: Capture), True
Sight: Conquest, and Forte: Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice — Swap Cancelling all of them makes her invulnerable
during those windows and is the foundation of her Dual-DPS value, but is mechanically demanding. None of
her Swap Cancels beyond those already in the sample rotations are REQUIRED to clear any content — the
most valuable to learn beyond the basics are her True Sight: Conquests specifically.

**Max Potential Rotation** (speedrun-tier, explicitly NOT recommended for casual play — "please take
good care of your hands"): Skill (swap) → True Sight: Conquest (swap) → Intro → True Sight: Charge →
Skill (swap) → True Sight: Conquest (swap) → Skill (swap) → True Sight: Conquest (swap) → Forte: Heavy:
Flaming Sacrifice (swap) → Ultimate → Forte: Heavy: Flaming Sacrifice (swap) → Outro. (In teams where
Skill cooldowns don't allow a 3rd Skill cast, use an extra Basic chain and only 2 Skills instead.)

**Synergies**: Aemeath/Brant (her two best partners, same Fusion-damage role, different execution —
Brant buffs her via Outro + brings healing/shielding + easy to pilot with strong personal damage;
Aemeath only worth it played well in Quickswap, since her short Outro requires Aemeath to weave Basic
Attack chains during Changli's rotation to fully benefit — Mono Fusion with Lupa+Brant or Lupa+Aemeath
clears anything when played well). Lupa (best Mono Fusion buffer — massive Fusion ATK/DMG Bonus boosts
in 3-Fusion-character teams; generally DON'T put Changli on the receiving end of Lupa's Outro since she
deals little Basic Attack DMG, unless her own Outro is more valuable elsewhere, e.g. the Aemeath+Lupa
team). Zhezhi (can replace Brant in a hypercarry-style team via her 25% Skill DMG Amp Outro + flat
Energy gen + off-field Coordinated Attack damage — far from optimal, only recommended if you own her,
want hypercarry Changli, and don't own Brant). Chixia (currently the ONLY character able to fully
utilize Changli's Outro — 20% Fusion + 25% Liberation DMG Amp — combined with Changli's own good
personal damage for a strong, easier-to-play Dual DPS pair). Encore (good Main DPS supported by
Changli's Outro Fusion DMG Amp; combined personal damage clears content easily once learned — a step up
in difficulty from single-DPS teams). Xiangli Yao (the infamous hardcore Dual DPS pair — huge damage in
long swap-cancellable windows Changli exploits well, her 25% Liberation Amp buffs his Ultimate nuke;
monstrous damage when learned, not recommended for casual players). Cartethyia (probably her best
option outside Ciaccona, still very difficult — both characters' swap windows let them deal damage
simultaneously; timing her 25% Liberation Amp Outro with Cartethyia's Ultimate adds extra damage).
Jinhsi (famous for some of the highest Expert quickswap DPS in the game AND excellent Whimpering Wastes
clear speed — tremendous difficulty ask). Yangyang (fun, fast, fully F2P and quickswap-friendly duo —
still difficult to master but not as much as the others). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing
Supports slotting into any team — Verina: fast rotation, team-wide ATK%/15% DMG Amp; Shorekeeper:
12.5% Crit Rate/25% Crit DMG/25% ATK/15% DMG Amp, similarly easy to use).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Changli buffs Aemeath via Outro here, Lupa buffs Changli — ideally
Quickswap only if Aemeath lacks better teammates; use Brant in most other cases), Brant Dual DPS Team,
Expert Team (very difficult, pick the 2nd teammate by preference — Verina is always stronger paired
with Jinhsi specifically), Dual Fusion Team (Encore is stronger than Chixia but harder to play), Budget
Dual DPS Team (a budget option, still works well) — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this
extraction; team names/conditions are directly from the source text.
