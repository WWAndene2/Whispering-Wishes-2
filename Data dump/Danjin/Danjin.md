The next character (or another nearby team member activating an Outro) gains 23% Havoc DMG Deepen for

## Review

- DPS Tier (Hybrid): T3 (Tower of Adversity), T3 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: same, T3/T3.
- **Pros**: playable as Solo DPS or a more supportive Hybrid; supports both long and short rotations
  depending on need; as a Sword character, most players likely already own a 5★ weapon that works
  great for her; very good Main DPS potential with Sequences by 4★ standards; benefits heavily from
  Dodge Counters, making her practically (not just theoretically) strong; incredible parrying ability
  makes bosses easier.
- **Cons**: high-risk-high-reward, often at 1 HP away from death, inconsistent especially against
  multiple enemies; underwhelming damage at S0; currently outclassed by Sanhua as a Hybrid, and by many
  limited 5★s as a Main DPS.
- 4★ Havoc Sword functioning as either a Hybrid support or a genuine high-DPS unit depending on
  rotation length. Falls into BOTH meta niches (dealing damage AND buffing others) at once, at the cost
  of rapidly depleting HP — she'll hover near 1 HP for most of a long fight, even with a healer, when
  played as DPS (a lesser but still present issue as Hybrid).
- Ultimate is simple: small-AoE damage, mostly used to cancel animation endlag (especially after
  Intro) while generating Concerto Energy.
- Resonance Skill is her kit's core — no cooldown, but requires Basic Attacks first to reach its 2
  interesting combos: the "mark combo" (2 Skills at the end — from 2 Basic Attacks, a perfect Dodge
  Counter, or Intro, each followed by 2 Skills) applies a 20%-DMG-on-marked-target-for-12s mark, best
  used ASAP (typically right after Intro); the "long combo" (3 Basic Attacks then 3 Skills) is the
  slowest but generates the most Forte/Concerto/DPS, near-purely single-target — used after the mark is
  placed. (Both combos may optionally replace the first Basic Attack with a single Skill cast, treated
  as equivalent for simplicity.)
- Forte Circuit is her biggest hit, with surprisingly notable AoE, accessed by Heavy Attack once the
  Forte gauge is at least half-full (50 stacks) — a full gauge instead grants ~2.4x the half-full
  multiplier. Both tiers restore ~1/3 max HP and generate identical Concerto Energy — the half-full
  version suits Hybrid play (shorter rotations, faster buff access), the full version suits Main DPS
  play (higher sustained damage).
- Dodge Counters chained into 2 Skills are a great, less time-consuming substitute for the long combo —
  stronger damage and more Forte at the cost of more HP; use them whenever the opportunity arises.
- Outro amplifies Havoc DMG by 23% for 14s, making her the best damage buffer for Havoc Rover and a
  very close contender to Sanhua in Camellya teams — combined with one of the shortest Hybrid rotation
  times in the game, a very competent Havoc-DPS buffer, a role that stays relevant even for future
  characters.
- Scales extremely well with Sequences — some of the best 4★ Sequence value in the game, especially
  S6, which adds a team-wide 20% ATK buff on her Forte Heavy Attack's first part, further boosting her
  supportive value.
- Summary: not top-tier meta because she doesn't out-buff Sanhua (the frequent comparison — both are
  quick-rotation Sword amplifiers sharing the same S6 shape) or match top 5★ DPS damage, but remains a
  reliably solid pick that trivializes endgame content via fast in-and-out buffing (like Sanhua) while
  also offering a genuine Main DPS path when more of her kit is exploited.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Maintenance**: keep Incinerating Will active as consistently as possible via Basic P1 (or Skill:
Carmine Gleam) → Basic P2 → Skill: Crimson Erosion I → Crimson Erosion II.

**Damage Dealer Combo** (longer, maximizes Forte gauge for a full-power double Forte Heavy Attack):
Intro → Skill: Crimson Erosion I → II → Ultimate → Skill: Carmine Gleam → Basic P2 → P3 → Skill:
Sanguine Pulse I → II → III → Skill: Carmine Gleam → Basic P2 → P3 → Skill: Sanguine Pulse I → II → III
→ Heavy Attack: Chaoscleave (Full Power) → Heavy Attack: Scatterbloom (Full Power) → Echo (Swap Cancel)
→ Outro.

**Hybrid Combo** (easy, no strings attached, fast Concerto, generates Outro quickly without giving up
her whole kit): Intro → Skill: Crimson Erosion I → II → Ultimate → Skill: Carmine Gleam → Basic P2 → P3
→ Skill: Sanguine Pulse I → II → III → Heavy Attack: Chaoscleave (Partial Power) → Heavy Attack:
Scatterbloom (Partial Power) → Echo (Swap Cancel) → Outro.

**Fast Hybrid Combo** (fastest Concerto generation, more complex, sacrifices even more damage/kit):
Skill: Carmine Gleam → Echo (Swap Cancel) → Intro → Skill: Crimson Erosion I → II → Ultimate → Skill:
Carmine Gleam → Basic P2 → Skill: Crimson Erosion I → II → Heavy Attack: Chaoscleave Full (Partial
Power)(Swap Cancel).

**Synergies**: Phrolova (fills missing field time flexibly via long/short rotations, S6 gives a nice
team ATK buff, good personal damage, amplifies Phrolova's Havoc DMG via Outro — one of Phrolova's best
4★ pairings). Camellya (one of Danjin's best Main DPS to support in Hybrid mode — huge Havoc DMG,
biggest AoE in the game; a great pair, though Danjin falls slightly behind Roccia/Sanhua as her
buffer). Rover: Havoc (flexible and strong in both Hypercarry and semi-quickswap Dual DPS styles,
thanks to Danjin's general Havoc DMG buff working well with Rover: Havoc's mixed damage types).
Cantarella (the first character to properly enable Danjin as a potent Main DPS — replaces the imperfect
Mortefi alternative by letting Danjin exploit multiple damage types via Cantarella's Skill + Havoc DMG
Amp Outro; together, the best 4★ Main DPS team in the game — especially with Danjin's S6 — approaching
5★ strength, though Danjin's HP-loss mechanic is especially punishing in this role, requiring real
practice). Roccia (great for Main DPS Danjin — solid personal damage, lots of grouping, a 20% Havoc +
25% Basic ATK DMG Amp Outro, plus 200 flat ATK on Ultimate). Mortefi (the budget Main-DPS-Danjin option
— amplifies Heavy Attacks, her main damage type in that role, by 38%, plus good off-field personal
damage and buffs via his Sequences — surprisingly synergistic thanks to Mortefi's S1). Verina/
Shorekeeper (generalist Healing Supports — Verina: fast rotation, team-wide ATK%/15% DMG Amp;
Shorekeeper: 12.5% Crit Rate/25% Crit DMG/25% ATK/15% DMG Amp).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (ideally run Cantarella in the 2nd slot, Outro-buffing Phrolova), Camellya
Team (Hybrid), Havoc Rover (Hybrid), Best Team (Main DPS) — recommended to pair Cantarella with Verina
here specifically, since Shorekeeper's extended field time can cause cooldown issues in this particular
team — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this extraction; team names/conditions are
directly from the source text.

**Full re-audit (2026-09-08, direct user request — "do the next one fully," including inner-kit and
cross-character interactions and explicit DOT-application/basis verification).** Two real bugs found
and fixed:

1. **`chain.s2` relied on `condition.requiresStance`, which the resolver never actually enforces.**
   `triggerEngine.js`'s own `conditionHolds()` treats `requiresStance` as purely descriptive except
   via a separate mutual-exclusion mechanism that doesn't apply here (the same bug class already
   found and fixed on Camellya's chain.s3/s6, which flagged it as a likely roster-wide pattern worth
   checking elsewhere). Left as an unscoped passive, this +20% `totalMult` (uncategorized — reaches
   EVERY hit) was silently applying to her pre-mark hits too (Intro, and the very Crimson Erosion cast
   that applies the mark), not just genuine Incinerating-Will-target hits. Retargeted to a real
   cast-anchored window opening on the Crimson Erosion cast, with a duration matching Incinerating
   Will's own real 12s lifetime — since her whole rotation is only 7.61s, this correctly excludes just
   her Intro hit while still covering the rest of the rotation once the mark is applied.
2. **Scatterbloom (179%) had no block anywhere**, despite `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS['Danjin']`'s own step
   note for the Chaoscleave step already saying "unleash Chaoscleave...into the Scatterbloom
   follow-up" — a real, guaranteed follow-up move (not optional/skipped), with its own dedicated
   `SKILL_MULTIPLIERS` row, "considered Heavy Attack DMG" per its own kit text. The dump's own Damage
   Profile "Heavy 24.4% (46,529)" bucket is consistent with the COMBINED Chaoscleave+Scatterbloom
   total (596.55% ATK), not Chaoscleave alone (417.55%) — confirming this was always meant to be
   counted. Folded into `danjin.forte.chaoscleave`'s own hit list (same category, same rotation step).
