# Denia — Build Guide (cleaned dump)

Last updated on source: 20/August/2026. Last review update: Patch 3.3. Last major build/calcs update: Patch 3.5.

5★ Fusion Rectifier. Dual-mode Fusion Hybrid (Stagecraft Form / Breakdown Form via her two Ultimates), plays into either the Fusion Burst or Tune Strain team archetype depending on Resonance Mode (configurable pre-combat).

### Outro Skill — Unfinished Lies

- **Fusion Burst mode**: Fusion Burst DMG near the active Resonator is Amplified by 60% for 30s. Ends early on mode switch.
- **Tune Strain mode**: incoming Resonator's All DMG Amplified by 15% for 16s. If that Resonator inflicts Tune Strain - Shifting under this effect, it jumps to 40% DMG Amplification instead. Ends early on swap-out or mode switch.

### Forte Circuit — "Flawless" / Shattered Hours

- **Erosion Field**: after casting Final Act - Breakdown Form, generates a 30s field (only 1 own-Resonator field at a time) that pulls in nearby targets every 4s, Fusion DMG, **considered Resonance Liberation DMG**. Multiplier: 136.33% per tick.
- **Void Particle** (Breakdown Form, while holding Void Particle): Normal Attack consumes Void Particle on hit — that Normal Attack's DMG is considered Resonance Liberation DMG, +50% DMG Multiplier; Conformal Charge regens 100% faster.
- **Resonance Mode - Fusion Burst**: Intro/Final Act (both forms)/Erosion Field inflict 2 stacks of Fusion Burst (once/2s/target); Basic Stage 3/4 (both forms)/Mid-air Stage 3/4 inflict 1 stack (once/2s/target). Fusion Burst kills near Denia count as the nearest active Resonator's kill.
- **Resonance Mode - Tune Strain**: same trigger list inflicts Tune Strain - Shifting and resets Tune Strain - Interfered duration (once/2s/target). Team inflicting Tune Strain - Shifting raises the target's Off-Tune Level by 50% of max (once per target per 300s).
- **Dark Core**: holds up to 3 (5 at S3). Gains 1 every 12s while in Entropy Shift. Intro skills each grant 1.
- **Void Particle** (gauge, up to 100): Stagecraft-Form Normal Attack hits grant it; Intro/Phantom Bubble each grant 25.
- **Conformal Charge** (gauge, up to 100): Breakdown-Form Normal Attack/Beckon hits grant it; Banish Stage 2 grants 40.
- **Shattered Hours**: at full Off-Tune Level, can cast Tune Break. Tune Strain mode: can inflict Tune Strain - Shifting, can respond to Tune Strain - Interfered (each stack × each point of Tune Break Boost = +0.12% total DMG vs. target). +1 to the target's max Tune Strain - Interfered stack cap while Denia is in the team.

### Inherent Skills

- **Vestiges of Falsehood**: entering combat in Stagecraft Form restores Dark Core to 2 (if below) and Void Particle to 20 (if below). Once/12s.
- **Etched Colors**: while in Entropy Shift — Fusion Burst mode: team +30% Fusion DMG Bonus. Tune Strain mode: team +10 Tune Break Boost; each 10% a Resonator's Off-Tune Buildup Rate exceeds 100% adds +8 more Tune Break Boost, up to +40.

## Gameplay and Teams

### Standard Rotation

Intro → Basic: Stagecraft 4 (cancel → Skill) → Skill: Phantom Bubble (cancel → Ultimate) → Ultimate: Stagecraft → Basic: Breakdown 1 → Basic: Breakdown 2 (cancel → Jump) → Jump → Basic: Breakdown Midair 1 → Basic: Breakdown Midair 2 (cancel → Skill) → Skill: Banish 1 → Skill: Banish 2 (cancel → Ultimate) → Ultimate: Breakdown → Outro.

Echo timing: any point in the rotation (Fusion Burst, Reminiscence: Denia/Lioness of Glory), or swap-cancelled right before switching out following Ultimate: Breakdown via Outro (Tune Strain, Voidwing Moth).

Easier variant: 4 plain Basic Attacks in Breakdown Form with no Jump Cancel (slightly slower, extra Fusion Burst/Tune Strain instances).

**Opener Rotation** (used once only, when Denia rotates first — e.g. Aemeath + Lupa + Denia): Basic: Stagecraft 1 (cancel → Skill) → Skill: Phantom Bubble (cancel → Dash) → Dash → Basic: Stagecraft 1 → Basic: Stagecraft 2 → Basic: Stagecraft 3 → Basic: Stagecraft 4 (cancel → Ultimate) → Ultimate: Stagecraft → Basic: Breakdown 1 → Basic: Breakdown 2 (cancel → Jump) → Jump → Basic: Breakdown Midair 1 → Basic: Breakdown Midair 2 (cancel → Skill) → Skill: Banish 1 → Skill: Banish 2 (cancel → Ultimate) → Ultimate: Breakdown → Outro.

S3 lets Denia skip directly to Breakdown Form on swap-in (Intro → Ultimate in Standard, or start with Ultimate in Opener), saving 1-2.7s. She's also quickswap-friendly during Breakdown Form basics (can swap out after Basic 2 and swap back later for 3/4).

### Synergies

- **Aemeath** — best and only DPS choice for Fusion Burst Denia; together one of the strongest teams in the game (AoE and single-target), fast/easy rotation, low weapon dependency. Stranded for Supports — only Chisa and Lupa unlock the team's full potential.
- **Qingxiao / Luuk Herssen** — Denia in Tune Strain mode is BiS partner for both, **if Mornye is available**. Significant team DPS drop-off without Mornye — not recommended for Tune Strain without her.
- **Chisa / Mornye / Lupa** — 3rd-slot options depending on team. Chisa ideal with Aemeath; Lupa a close fallback (own Echo set, ~same performance). Mornye best for Tune Strain teams, provides nothing to Aemeath's team.

### Example Teams

- **Fusion Burst**: Aemeath / Denia / Chisa / Suisui / Lupa. With Lupa: Denia on Flaming Clawprint, Lupa on Moonlit Clouds; Denia rotates first, Outros into Lupa who Outros into Aemeath.
- **Tune Strain**: Qingxiao / Luuk Herssen / Denia / Mornye. No listed alternative to Mornye — significant DPS drop-off without her.

## Review

**Ratings — Hybrid**: Tower of Adversity T0, Whimpering Wastes T0.5. Value Tier List: ToA (Value) T0.5, WW (Value) T1.

**Pros**: part of 2 of the best non-QuickSwap teams and the best QuickSwap team in the game (both Fusion Burst and Tune Strain); many viable weapon alts in Fusion Burst mode; constant off-field grouping; fast rotation, high damage, strong buffs/utility.

**Cons**: only 2 team comps, both Support-limited — no flexibility without either team; heavily Signature-weapon-dependent in Tune Strain mode; needs 3 different Echo Sets across her 3 main team options.

Resonance Mode changes: her "Etched Colors" Inherent Skill payoff, most attacks' Fusion Burst-vs-Tune Strain application, a once-per-battle 50%-max Tune Break gauge fill (Tune Strain only), and her Outro's payoff (Fusion Burst DMG Amp vs. general DMG Amp).

Key mechanic: her actual combat focus is unlocking her second Ultimate ("Final Act - Breakdown Form") by cycling Stagecraft Form (fills the Breakdown gauge) into Breakdown Form (spends it, deals the enhanced damage, then re-enters Stagecraft via the loop). Rotation grants: Fusion Burst application + off-field grouping + Fusion Burst DMG Amp (Outro) + Fusion DMG Bonus (Echo Set/Inherent Skill) in Fusion Burst mode; Tune Strain application/refresh + off-field grouping + general DMG Amp (Outro) + Tune Break Boost/ATK% (Echo set/Inherent Skill) in Tune Strain mode. Signature weapon also buffs team ATK.

Meta position: as of release, only 2 teams exist for her (Aemeath for Fusion Burst, Luuk Herssen/Qingxiao + Mornye for Tune Strain), no guaranteed additional teams coming. Both are very strong in the current meta and work in AoE and single-target. Recommended pickup if you already run Aemeath or Luuk/Qingxiao without a premium team.

## Full re-audit (2026-09-08)

Full, independent re-verification of `denia.blocks.js` against this dump, `characters.js` (CHAR_BUFF_TABLE, RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA, SKILL_MULTIPLIERS, CHARACTER_ROTATIONS, full CHARACTER_DATA entry), and the engine's actual resolver code (not comments) — per the same rigor applied to Augusta through Danjin this session. Three real bugs found and fixed; everything else cross-checked and confirmed correct.

**Bug 1 — `denia.chain.s6` silently unconditional the whole rotation.** Was `trigger:{type:'passive'}`, gated only by kit text ("while in Entropy Shift") with no enforcement mechanism. Entropy Shift doesn't exist until her first Final Act (Ultimate) cast, but her modeled `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS['Denia']` opens with 3 real pre-Ultimate hits (Intro, Basic Stagecraft Stage 1, Phantom Bubble) — all 3 were silently getting +60% ATK/+60% Fusion DMG Bonus they shouldn't. Retargeted to `trigger:{type:'cast', on:'Liberation:Final Act: Stagecraft Form'}` (her rotation always casts Final Act - Stagecraft Form first), `timing.duration:99` (sentinel — Entropy Shift persists, mode-switching, through both Ultimates to the rotation's end).

**Bug 2 — `denia.inherent.etched-colors-fusion-burst` (team +30% Fusion DMG Bonus) used an unenforced `condition.requiresStance`.** Confirmed directly in `triggerEngine.js`'s `conditionHolds()`: `requiresStance` is purely descriptive except via a separate mutual-exclusion mechanism that doesn't apply here — the same bug class already found and fixed on Camellya's chain.s3/s6 and Danjin's chain.s2. This team-wide buff was silently active from combat start. Retargeted to the same cast anchor as chain.s6 above, for the same reason.

**Bug 3 — Intro and both Final Act casts missing `dotApplier` tags.** This dump's own kit text (Forte Circuit section, above) names Intro/Final Act (both forms)/Erosion Field as the 4 real Fusion-Burst appliers in one sentence, but only Erosion Field carried a `dotApplier` tag feeding the cross-character Fusion Burst reactivity system (`dotReactionsFromBlocks.js`, purely tag-driven, no fallback). Added matching `dotApplier: { mechanic: 'fusionBurst', requiresStance: 'Fusion Burst mode', value: 2 }` to `denia.intro.its-been-a-while`, `denia.liberation.final-act-stagecraft`, and `denia.liberation.final-act-breakdown`.

**Disclosed gap, not fixed (flagged, no schema support exists):** S1's own kit text ("Entering combat in Stagecraft Form grants Entropy Shift: Stagecraft Form (30s)") has no matching "combat entry" trigger type in this engine schema — not modeled. This makes the chain.s6/etched-colors-fusion-burst cast-anchored fix above exactly correct for Sequence 0-5, but a known imprecision at Sequence 6 specifically (since sequences are cumulative — S6 implies S1 is also owned, so a real S6 Denia's Entropy Shift is active from combat start, not from her first Final Act cast). Documented inline in `denia.chain.s1`'s note.

**Verified, no bug found:**
- `statScaling`/`basis` consistency: `CHARACTER_DATA['Denia'].statScaling` is `'ATK'`; all 10 damage blocks use `basis: 'ATK'` — no mismatch (the Baizhi-class bug does not recur here).
- `SKILL_MULTIPLIERS['Denia']` percentages match every damage block's hit list exactly (Basic ATK Stagecraft/Breakdown, Skill, Liberation, Intro, Outro rows all cross-checked).
- `RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA['Denia']` (S1-S6) and `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE['Denia']` (outro buffs, fusionBurst debuff flag, tuneBreak block) match their block-file counterparts exactly — both tables already carry their own prior audit comments (2026-08-31/2026-09-02) that were independently re-verified against this dump's kit text, not just trusted.
- `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS['Denia']` (the real, actively-used rotation data) matches `denia.blocks.js`'s cast-anchor choices exactly (Final Act - Stagecraft Form always first) — confirming the chain.s6/etched-colors-fusion-burst fix's cast-anchor choice is correct for the actual modeled rotation.
- `CHARACTER_DATA['Denia'].rotation` (the older, unused plain-string legacy field — confirmed dead via `calcTeamStats.js`'s own comment: `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS` is preferred whenever present, and it is here) is stale/inconsistent with the real rotation (mentions "Dash" and omits the midair Breakdown segment) but has zero functional effect on any calculation — left as-is per Change Scope Discipline (an unrelated dead-field cleanup, not part of this fix).
