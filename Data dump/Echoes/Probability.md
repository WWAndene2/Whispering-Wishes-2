# Echo Stats — Probability Data

Source: reference page "Echo/Stats" page. Pasted verbatim by the user 2026-09-06.

## Classification

Echoes can belong to one of four Classes: Common (Cost 1), Elite (Cost 3), Overlord (Cost 4) and Calamity (Cost 4). Resonators are given an Echo Cost limit which depends on the player's current Data Bank Level. The total Cost from all the equipped Echoes on a character cannot exceed the player's Echo Cost Limit. The maximum achievable Echo Cost Limit is 12, which unlocks at Data Bank Level 9.

Echoes have different rarity. The rank of a captured Echo depends on the player's current Data Bank Level. Rarity and cost have no correlation. The maximum upgradable Level of an Echo depends on its rarity — this rule also applies to possible Tacet Field echo drops.

| Data Bank Level | Highest obtainable Echo Rarity | Highest upgradable Level of Echo |
|---|---|---|
| 0-4 | 2 | 10 |
| 5-7 | 3 | 15 |
| 8-14 | 4 | 20 |
| 15-25 | 5 | 25 |

## Substats

Substats are additional stats unlocked via Tuning. An Echo can have up to 5 Substats which cannot be changed or re-rolled. Substats roll independently of the main stats. The rolls are fixed and the chances appear to follow a Gaussian distribution. The number of possible rolls is 8 for every Substat aside from flat ATK and DEF, which have 4 rolls. The weight of substat drop rate (which substat gets picked, as opposed to which value grade it rolls) is currently unknown.
