## Review

- DPS Tier: T2 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T3 (ToA Value), T4 (WW
  Value).
- **Pros**: playable as Hyper Carry or in Quick-Swap double-damage teams; usable in far more teams than
  most characters thanks to her flexibility; very easy to pick up; incredible Resonance Energy
  generation for herself and the team; has a pet sheep.
- **Cons**: Forte-charged Heavy Attacks have a small input window that can leave her vulnerable before
  she can switch; limited parrying potential outside Ultimate form; more challenging-to-use Echo
  options than other elements.
- 5★ Fusion Rectifier, playable as sole-DPS Hyper Carry (stack buffs before Ultimate for a burst
  rotation) or in Quick-Swap double-DPS teams (keep Skill/Echo on cooldown while cycling teammates,
  Ultimate mostly filling downtime between other characters' cooldowns).
- Fully-charged Forte turns Heavy Attack into a high-DPS, long-cast attack — enhanced further during
  Ultimate, should always be cast then; despite the long cast it CAN be Swap-Cancelled (should always
  be), though there's a small vulnerable window right before the cancel is available.
- Skill has 2 parts: a long barrage (Flaming Woolies, decent DPS + large Energy gen) and a rarely-used
  2nd-part explosion (Energetic Welcome, good damage but poor Energy gen) that must be triggered
  immediately after Flaming Woolies — usually Swap-Cancelled away to avoid its long cast.
- Ultimate converts her from ranged mage to melee attacker, empowering most abilities — most of her
  damage in this state comes from spamming Basic Attacks.
- One of the easiest ToA characters in the game: forgiving Quick-Swap Skill, forgiving burst rotation,
  built-in gap-closers on most abilities mean mashing attacks + dodging gets reasonable results. The
  only hard part is cleanly landing the Forte-enhanced Heavy Attack and timing the Inferno Rider switch.
- Swap-Cancellation is essential regardless of build style; she always uses a 5-piece damage-focused
  Echo set in every team.
- Solid Hyper Carry option in endgame, even stronger in Quick-Swap teams thanks to flexible field time.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Warm-up** (use on cooldown while waiting/quickswapping): Skill P1 Flaming Woolies, Swap-Cancelled
immediately after cast. Echo on cooldown (especially Inferno Rider, Swap-Cancelled as needed — keep its
buff active through her whole Ultimate duration when she has field time to spare). Charged Heavy Attack
whenever Forte (Mayhem) is full, Swap-Cancelled immediately — align with her Ultimate whenever possible
for a big damage increase.

**Easy & Basic Burst Combo**: Echo → Intro → Ultimate → Skill: Cosmos: Rampage → Basic P1-4 (Cosmos:
Frolicking) → Skill: Cosmos: Rampage → Basic P1-4 → Skill: Cosmos: Rampage → Heavy: Cosmos: Rupture
(Swap-Cancel the instant the channel begins) → Outro. In Quick-Swap teams, ending the Ultimate early to
cast Rupture before its duration ends can be worthwhile (generally NOT advised if she's the sole
buffed DPS).

**Advanced Burst Combo**: same opener, but trades one enhanced Skill cast for 4 more Basic Attacks by
cancelling the 4th enhanced Basic Attack's animation (via Skill or Dash) 3 times during the Ultimate —
higher damage, much tighter execution window.

**No Forte Rotation** (theoretical best, hardest to execute): forfeits the Forte Heavy Attack entirely
for a 3rd Cosmos: Rampage cast, then swaps back in after Outro specifically to cast Energetic Welcome
before swapping out again (a steep damage loss to skip). Advantage: since it never needs Dodge
Counters, Ultimate duration can be tracked purely by Skill cooldown (3rd Skill back up ≈ Ultimate about
to end). The Forte Heavy Attack CAN still be performed off-field in longer-rotation teams (Mono Fusion/
Lupa teams).

**Synergies**: Lupa (now Encore's new best teammate over Sanhua in most situations — bigger Fusion DMG
buffs + an extra 7% DMG Amp, and fills the downtime Sanhua+Encore's fast rotations otherwise leave for
a 3rd teammate). Sanhua (one of her strongest teammates regardless — tiny field time, 38% Basic Attack
DMG Amp on Outro, strong S6/Echo-Set buffs, complementary swap-cancel windows; low personal damage is
barely an issue). Changli (complicated but workable — Encore benefits from her 20% Fusion DMG Outro
buff). Brant (better than the Changli pairing thanks to Skill DMG Amp Outro + general Fusion DMG Amp +
strong personal damage — put him in the 2nd slot buffed by Lupa). Mornye/Shorekeeper/Verina (best
generalist Supports — Mornye has special synergy with Encore+Lupa, letting Encore run Hyper Carry while
Lupa keeps her full Mono Fusion buffs; Shorekeeper's massive Crit buffs are a strong alternative;
Verina is the most accessible strong option if neither is available).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, With Sanhua, Best Team (Dual DPS) — exact roster icons weren't
machine-readable in this extraction; names/conditions are directly from the source text.

## Full re-audit (2026-09-08)

Full, independent re-verification of `encore.blocks.js` against this dump, `characters.js` (CHAR_BUFF_TABLE,
RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA, SKILL_MULTIPLIERS, CHARACTER_ROTATIONS, full CHARACTER_DATA entry), and the
engine's actual resolver code (not comments) — per the same rigor applied to Augusta through Denia this
session. Two real precision bugs found and fixed (both the same under-counting class, verified by
direct measurement, not assumption); everything else re-verified and confirmed correct.

**Bug 1 — `encore.chain.s1` under-counted its own real cap.** The real mechanic is "+3% Fusion DMG per
Basic Attack HIT, stacking up to 4 times (12% max) for 6s". The block was anchored to the whole-combo
CAST of Cosmos: Frolicking (`stacking:'stacking', maxStacks:4`), but that whole-combo cast only fires
twice in the real modeled rotation — the engine's `activeCountAt()` counts concurrently-active 6s
windows opened by real firings of the exact cast trigger, and with only 2 real cast events (their
windows overlapping), it never sees more than 2 concurrent windows: capped at 2/4 stacks (6%), never
the real 12%. The real per-sub-hit trigger (a single Frolicking cast lands 12 real sub-hits) reaches
the cap within the first few hits of the very first cast. No "any Basic-ATK sub-hit" trigger type
exists in this engine, so — same established pattern as Danjin's S1 dead-stacking-metadata fix —
retargeted to a flat value (12%, the real cap) from the first Frolicking cast onward (sentinel
duration, since dense ongoing Basic-ATK activity keeps refreshing the real stacks through the rest of
the rotation), instead of a stacking mechanic tied to an under-firing anchor.

**Bug 2 — `encore.chain.s6` under-counted its own real cap, same class.** The real mechanic is "1 stack
of Lost Lamb per damage instance during Cosmos Rave, each +5% ATK, stacking up to 5 times (25% max) for
10s". The block was anchored to the Cosmos: Rampage CAST specifically (3 real casts in the modeled
rotation), capping at 3/5 stacks (15%), never the real 25% — dozens of real sub-hits (Rampage + both
Frolicking combos + Cosmos Rupture) land within the single 10s Cosmos Rave window, saturating the real
5-stack cap almost instantly. Retargeted to a flat value (25%, the real cap) anchored to the
Liberation:Cosmos Rave cast itself (the window's own real opening event, correctly zero before it),
sentinel duration matching the same "persists through dense ongoing activity" reasoning as Bug 1.

Both bugs verified via direct measurement (not assumption): confirmed the old anchor's real firing
count in the modeled rotation (2 Frolicking casts, 3 Rampage casts) against the stack durations/spacing
before concluding the cap was never reached, then added positive-verification tests proving both fixes
correctly zero-out pre-trigger hits (Intro) while boosting post-trigger hits (Cosmos Rupture).

**Verified, no bug found (re-confirmed by fresh independent test, not by trusting the prior comment):**
- The prior 2026-09-06 decision to omit `timing.cooldown` on `encore.skill.cosmos-rampage` despite a
  real sourced 4s cooldown value — re-tested independently this pass (added cooldown:4 back in an
  isolated diagnostic run): confirmed it drops only 8/12 real Cosmos: Rampage sub-hits instead of 12/12
  (2 of 3 real casts survive the engine's coarse per-step cooldown gate instead of all 3), reproducing
  the same real engine limitation the existing comment already described. Correctly left unmodeled.
- `statScaling`/`basis`: `CHARACTER_DATA['Encore'].statScaling` is `'ATK'`; every damage block uses
  `basis: 'ATK'` — no mismatch.
- DOT/dotApplier completeness: Encore's kit applies no Fusion Burst or other cross-character DOT status
  anywhere in her real kit text (Fusion DMG only, no "inflicts Fusion Burst" language anywhere in this

- `encore.selfbuff.angry-cosmos`'s `condition.requiresStance: 'HP above 70%'` — checked whether the
  real, ENFORCED `condition.casterHpPct` schema field (added 2026-09-05) should replace it. It should

  this resolver path — using it would silently zero the whole buff out, strictly worse than the current
  always-on approximation for a solo burst-DPS calc. Kept as-is; now documented inline with the full
  reasoning rather than left as an unexplained choice. This is the same simplification
  `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE['Encore']`'s own selfBuffs entry already discloses ("TODO: needs Phase 2 schema...
