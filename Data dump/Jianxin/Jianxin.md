## Review

**Ratings**: Hybrid — T4 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: one of the most diverse kits in the game, can perform every role to some degree; a
one-woman-army in the overworld (damage, shields, self-healing); best grouping in the game via her
Ultimate; one of the easiest characters to use; free parries can ignore certain boss mechanics.

**Cons**: ultimate jack-of-all-trades, no effective meta use aside from grouping; stands still during
Forte channeling, often losing team damage or shield; damaging weapon options are hard to acquire (not
necessary as support); can't do much useful with her Ultimate on cooldown; takes a lot of field time
for shields/buffs; free parry can prevent staggering enemies (especially bosses); survivability scales
heavily with investment, a problem for a support.

**Summary**: a 5★ Aero Gauntlets character that can fit DPS, Hybrid, or Support to varying degrees of
success — a true multi-purpose character, amazing for Overworld content (grouping, shields, self-heal,
passable damage). Worst as DPS, usable as Hybrid, mostly valued as Support/grouping in endgame. Her
Resonance Skill's Parry requires reading enemy attacks (little advantage over a regular dodge). Her
Outro's 38% Liberation DMG Amp is huge for Calcharo/Xiangli Yao, but her poor Concerto generation and
Outro's swap-incompatibility limit its use. Her Forte grants 50% DMG reduction, self-heal, and a potent
shield — but its 6s channel is often interrupted by enemy movement or eats into rotation time; unusable
in Hologram fights without Bell-Borne Geochelone. Her Ultimate (pull + constant damage, ~3s) is the
cleanest part of her kit — costly Energy-wise but patchable via ER echoes. Finds a real niche as a
low-investment grouping support for the Tower of Adversity's middle floors (via Originite: Type IV +
Rejuvenating Glow). Summary: excellent comfort Hybrid for the Overworld, but spread too thin to excel
anywhere specific in endgame content.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Rotation guidance** (Support focus): keep Resonance Skill on cooldown unless it interrupts a higher
priority; keep Basic Attacking to maintain 5P Rejuvenating Glow/Originite: Type IV uptime (also builds
Concerto); use Echo as often as possible outside a pre-burst save; always use her Ultimate for Concerto
and enemy control, saving it to group at the right moment; align her Outro with the main DPS's burst or
Ultimate when possible; use her Forte Gauge as needed — almost always a net damage loss, but sometimes
worth it for the Shield/Concerto trade.

**Synergies**: Iuno (her best DPS to buff — 38% Liberation DMG Amp on an overwhelming majority of
Iuno's damage; also benefits from Aero Rover's Aero DMG Amp for a strong F2P-accessible mono-Aero team
if Ciaccona isn't available). Mortefi (peculiar synergy — with his Ultimate cast and her Forte ready,
she can trigger many Marcato Coordinated Attacks, quickly refilling Jinhsi's Incandescence for a viable
F2P Jinhsi team). Xiangli Yao (a primary DPS to buff, a good alternative to Yinlin, though he'll need
more Energy Regen and get less buffing/less field-time efficiency than with Yinlin). Verina/Shorekeeper
(generalist Healing Supports).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, F2P Jinhsi Team, Xiangli Yao Team — exact roster icons weren't
machine-readable in this extraction; team names are directly from the source text.
