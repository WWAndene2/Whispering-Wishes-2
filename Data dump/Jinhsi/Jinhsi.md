## Review

- DPS Tier: T2 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T2 (ToA Value), T4 (WW
  Value) — same as standard Ratings this time.
- **Pros**: large mid-air combat focus — hard to hit, missing attacks is a non-issue; huge Swap/
  Animation Cancel room, extremely optimizable; can get Outros off extremely quickly, multiple per
  rotation via her unique passives; can capitalize on under-utilized F2P teammates (Yuanwu) and is one
  of the best users of Verina — teammates always accessible.
- **Cons**: no good new teammates since 1.3 — Coordinated Attacks is a dead archetype since 2.2, very
  outdated in teams/power level; getting interrupted mid-air completely ruins her rotation speed (falls
  tumbling); signature weapon is close to a minimum requirement for modern endgame; 5P Celestial
  Light's set bonus needs regular Intros even though her kit could theoretically work without them, and
  her Skill nuke's Coordinated-Attack-fed empowerment limits both teammate options and rotation speed;
  completely unusable in multi-wave content (Whimpering Wastes) no matter how optimized, barring a
  top-tier carry.
- 5★ Spectro Broadblade Main DPS, historically the strongest 1.x DPS on release, defined the early meta
  via accessible teammates and ease of use. Playstyle emphasizes Animation/Swap Cancelling, mid-air
  combat, and huge AoE nukes.
- Key mechanics: Forte gauge (Incandescence, cap 50) is the focus of her whole kit — nearly everything
  else exists to feed it. Gained via Eras in Unity from ANY team member's Attribute DMG (1 stack, 3s
  cooldown per Attribute) or Coordinated Attack (2 stacks, its own 3s cooldown per Attribute) — after
  her Outro, both cooldowns drop from 3s to 1s for 20s. Illuminous Epiphany (accessed after Incarnation
  Basic Attack Stage 4) is empowered massively by Incandescence stacks — at full power one of the
  hardest-hitting attacks in the game. Every 25s she gets a FREE Outro (no Concerto cost) after casting
  this Skill. Incarnation itself is entered via Overflowing Radiance (instant off Intro, or slower off
  Basic Attack Stage 4).
- Rotation concepts: highly variable by team comp — can frontload or backload damage, mostly
  concentrated in her empowered Skill and Ultimate (making her a strong crit-fisher: build lower Crit
  Rate/higher Crit DMG and reset runs until the 2 key hits crit). Free Outro every 25s is designed
  around casting 2 Skills per loop — Jinhsi (and her Hybrid) rotate twice per full team loop while the
  Support rotates once. Rotation speed is thankfully easy to keep under 30s (preserving team buff
  uptime). Managing Forte-stack buildup so the NEXT Skill nuke isn't overcapped (losing damage) is her
  main speed constraint — otherwise she's free to rotate as fast as desired.
- Meta position: uniquely high skill ceiling most inexperienced players underrate, but genuinely
  powercrept — teammate stagnation since v1.2-1.3 limits her Hypercarry ceiling in ToA specifically, and
  she's fundamentally unsuited to Whimpering Wastes by design (all damage concentrated in a few nukes
  she tends to overkill enemies with). Still serviceable in Tower/Matrix for committed players.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Key mechanics recap**: via Unison, casting her Forte Skill (after Incarnation-Basic-Attack Stage 4)
grants a free Outro every 25s with no Concerto buildup required — she also still separately fills
Concerto Energy for a 2nd Outro path. Every 2 Forte Skill casts + 1 Ultimate nets her standard Outro,
while Unison itself procs every OTHER Forte Skill cast — meaning she can get 2 Outros per rotation
cycle, and doesn't block her 2nd buffer from Introing into her twice either. She wants to build 2
Intros + 2 Outros per rotation cycle: 2 Forte Skill nukes + 1 Ultimate, then rotate back to her general
Support.

**Standard Rotation** (Opener, no swap/animation cancels): Basic P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 → Skill: Overflowing
Radiance → Ultimate → Incarnation Basic P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 → Skill: Illuminous Epiphany → Outro.
**Loop Rotation** (repeats after opener, Outro now accelerates Incandescence gen): Intro → Skill:
Overflowing Radiance → Ultimate → Incarnation Skill: Crescent Divinity → Incarnation Basic P1-4 →
Skill: Illuminous Epiphany → Outro. (Complete a 2nd-character rotation here.) Intro → Skill: Overflowing
Radiance → Incarnation Skill: Crescent Divinity → Incarnation Basic P1-4 → Skill: Illuminous Epiphany →
Outro.

**Advanced Optimizations** (Animation Cancels — low effort, most players should do this): Incarnation
Basic Attack always advances stages even if interrupted. Ultimate instantly cancels ANY attack (0
damage on the cancelled hit, but skips straight ahead toward the Forte Skill). Dash and base Crescent
Divinity also cancel attacks. Overflowing Radiance instantly cancels Basic P4 (0 damage, faster
Incarnation entry); Illuminous Epiphany instantly cancels Incarnation Basic P4 too, but retains its
full damage. Swapping into Jinhsi from another in-combat character (no Intro) starts her Basic combo at
P2, not P1.
**Advanced Opener**: swap in → Basic P2 → P3 → P4 (Instant Cancel) → Skill: Overflowing Radiance
(Instant Cancel) → Ultimate → Incarnation Basic P1 → P2 (Dash Cancel) → P3 (Dash Cancel) → P4 (Instant
Cancel) → Skill: Illuminous Epiphany → Outro.
**Advanced Loop Rotation**: Intro → Skill: Overflowing Radiance (Dash Cancel) → Incarnation Basic P1 →
P2 → P3 (Instant Cancel) → Skill: Crescent Divinity → Incarnation Basic P4 (Instant Cancel) → Skill:
Illuminous Epiphany → Outro. (2nd-character rotation here.) Intro → Skill: Overflowing Radiance
(Instant Cancel) → Ultimate → Incarnation Basic P1 → P2 → P3 (Instant Cancel) → Skill: Crescent Divinity
→ Incarnation Basic P4 (Instant Cancel) → Skill: Illuminous Epiphany → Outro.

**Expert Optimizations** (Swap & Jump Cancels): a Mid-Air Attack can be squeezed in right after
Incarnation ends and before swapping out, at no rotation-time cost. Her Echo (Jué) generates an updraft
on cast, usable to trigger the Glider at very low height — chained correctly in the Opener (ground-
started Incarnation Basics), this gets her to Illuminous Epiphany in just a couple of seconds; her
hardest technique to learn (precise positioning + fast inputs), only realistic in the Opener. To finish
a boss fast, she can skip Crescent Divinity entirely and Dash Cancel Incarnation Basic P2/P3 instead,
same as the Opener. Without desirable Outro buffs from teammates, she can build Forte entirely without
Intros by weaving Basic 2-3-4 + Overflowing Radiance into another character's animation before
switching out — but Echo/weapon buffs still apply regardless (best to cast her Forte Skills within 15s
of an Intro when possible). Her Overflowing Radiance and Crescent Divinity casts are her most notable
Swap Cancel windows.
**Expert Opener**: swap in → Basic P2 → P3 → P4 (Instant Cancel) → cast Jué (updraft) → Skill:
Overflowing Radiance (Instant Cancel) → Ultimate → Incarnation Basic P1 → P2 (Jump Cancel) → P3 (Jump/
Glider Cancel) → P4 (Instant Cancel) → Skill: Illuminous Epiphany → Outro — all within roughly 2s.
**Expert Loop Rotation**: same as Advanced Loop, but with a Mid-Air Attack (Swap) inserted right before
each Outro.

**(Bonus) Holograms Grapple Technique**: exclusively for Holograms (or emergency damage avoidance) —
let Overflowing Radiance's animation nearly finish (sends her to peak height), then Grapple, going high
enough that most enemy attacks can't reach her; complete the Basic Attack chain and Forte Skill safely
mid-air. Does NOT work with Crescent Divinity (it instantly grounds her) — use it right after Overflowing
Radiance before grappling, or skip it entirely that rotation.

**Synergies**: Zhezhi/Yinlin/Cantarella (her premium Coordinated-Attacker options — Zhezhi generally
best, buffing Skill DMG on Outro + funneling Energy + very high Coordinated Attack uptime; Yinlin great
for quickswap, near-permanent Coordinated Attack uptime single-wave but struggles multi-wave; Cantarella
workable only with temporary Echo-Skill-favoring endgame buffs — her strict 25s Ultimate/Forte cooldown

Shorekeeper gives the highest generalist buffing via unique Crit buffs, Verina's fast rotation + own
Coordinated Attacks funnel her Skills easily; near-equal, difference comes down to Crit consistency).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Cantarella swappable for Zhezhi with temporary Echo-Skill-favoring
buffs; Verina can beat Shorekeeper if Crit consistency isn't an issue, otherwise Shorekeeper), Quickswap
Team (Verina must run with Brant+Changli for Coordinated Attacks — difficult, not recommended for

## Full kit audit (2026-09-08)

Full, independent re-verification of `jinhsi.blocks.js` against this dump, `characters.js`
(CHAR_BUFF_TABLE, RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA, SKILL_MULTIPLIERS, CHARACTER_ROTATIONS, full CHARACTER_DATA
entry), and the engine's actual resolver code (not comments) — per the same rigor applied to Augusta
through Jianxin this session. This is one of the most mechanically complex characters in the roster
(strict cast-order windows, Incarnation/Ordination Glow state, Unison-gated free Outros). Three real
findings, all verified by direct measurement rather than assumption; no DPS-affecting bug found, but
real documentation/hygiene gaps fixed.

**Finding 1 — the two `windowed-cast` "forfeit window" blocks are non-gating, contrary to the file's
own header claim.** The file's header comment described these as "this conversion's whole point," but
direct A/B measurement (removing both blocks from the block set) produces a byte-identical DPS total.
Root cause: the real damage blocks (`jinhsi.skill.overflowing-radiance`, `jinhsi.skill.illuminous-
epiphany`) each fire off their OWN independent `cast` trigger, completely unaffected by whether the
sibling `windowed-cast` utility block's real elapsed-time check (`tryWindowedCast()`, confirmed to be a
real, working implementation, not a stub) passes or fails — and even a successful check contributes
nothing, since both utility blocks carry `effects:[]`. Nothing else in the codebase reads these block
ids either. Given this engine's established "assume optimal execution, never model a missed window"
convention (used for every other character), and `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS['Jinhsi']` already representing
the always-in-window ideal case, this has zero effect on the real, correct DPS number — but the file's
own claim about what the mechanism accomplishes was wrong. Corrected the header and both blocks' own
notes to accurately describe them as documentation-only.

**Finding 2 — S2's `totalMult: 5` was a fabricated, unexplained placeholder, doubly dead.** The real S2
effect ("staying out of combat 4s+ restores 50 Incandescence") is pure out-of-combat resource utility
with zero in-combat DPS component — the same class of fabricated non-zero value already found and
zeroed for Iuno's S4 and Jianxin's S1-S3/S5 ("zero, don't guess" rule), but this one was never caught in
Jinhsi's own pass. Independently confirmed the value was ALSO already dead for an unrelated reason: the
block's own `kind:'utility'` excludes it from every effect-processing list in
`resolveHitComposedDps.js` (`passiveBlocks`/`buffBlocks`/`instantCastBuffBlocks` all require
`kind === 'buff' || 'debuff'`), so the `totalMult:5` never contributed regardless of its value — the old
note's claim that it was "kept as... a placeholder" implied it did something; it never did. Zeroed both
`RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA['Jinhsi'].s2` and the block's own `effects` to remove the confusing fabricated
number (no functional DPS change, since it was already contributing zero).

**Finding 3 — S3's real Resonance Chain bonus contributes zero, even at Sequence 6, undisclosed on the
block itself.** `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS['Jinhsi']` (the "Standard Rotation (Opener)" per this dump) never
casts Intro at all — confirmed directly and by measurement (removing `jinhsi.chain.s3-celestial-
incarnate` produces a byte-identical total even at Sequence 6). Her real "Loop Rotation" (this dump's
own text) DOES cast Intro every cycle after the opener, so a real S6 Jinhsi's actual in-game DPS
benefits from this +50% ATK far more than this calculator currently shows — this is a genuine
consequence of the engine modeling only her opener, not a data error on this specific node, but it was
previously undisclosed on chain.s3 itself (only implicitly buried in the file's Intro-related header
note about the missing damage block). Documented explicitly on the block.

**Also fixed (hygiene):** added a documentation-only `jinhsi.outro.temporal-bender` utility block —
`CHARACTER_ROTATIONS['Jinhsi']` includes a real `Outro:Temporal Bender` step, but this file previously
had no block for it at all (the real effect is a pure Incandescence-gain-rate accelerator with zero
team-DMG-buff/DPS-stat component, matching `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE['Jinhsi'].outroBuffs: []`).

**Verified, no bug found:**
