## Review

- DPS Tier: T1.5 (Tower of Adversity), T1.5 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier: T2 / T3.
- **Pros**: incredible burst damage during Ultimate; one of the best AoE DPS in the game; gathers and
  crowd-controls enemies constantly during Ultimate; parries most elite/boss enemies during his main
  combo; easy and straightforward; scales insanely well with Sequences.
- **Cons**: completely Ultimate-reliant (all meaningful damage happens during the burst window);
  clunky without correct cooldown/Energy Regen management (Ultimate won't be ready in time).
- Aero Broadblade DPS, excels at AoE, best in Hyper Carry teams since the majority of his damage is
  tied to the Ultimate burst window. Doesn't benefit from swap-canceling — such tactics cost him damage
  during the burst.
- Forte "Resolve" (cap 60) is charged via Basic Attacks + Intro. 30 Resolve (half bar) can either
  enhance Resonance Skill (+20% DMG) or the empowered Liberation (Finale, adds a massive draconic
  strike) — strongly recommended to save Resolve for the empowered Ultimate rather than the mediocre
  Skill enhancement.
- Resonance Skill: simple forward-dash spear thrust, decent Aero DMG, good Concerto/Resonance Energy
  generation.
- Ultimate → Qingloong Mode: enhances Basic/Heavy/Dodge Counter into the 3-part Lance of Qingloong
  combo (8 hits per part), summons a dragon, sweeps enemies into the air (excludes most elites, all
  bosses, rooted enemies), increased Anti-interruption.
  Spends his entire Ultimate duration on-field for max DPS; needs frequent swap-ins to keep casting his
  low-CD Skill for enough Resonance Energy to recast the Ultimate on time — requires Hyper Carry
  compositions due to this field-time need.
- Very easy to play at his ceiling: just ensure buffs are up before the Ultimate cast and Resonance
  Energy generation keeps pace.
- Currently one of the best DPS for Tower of Adversary, unlikely to phase out of meta given his
  combined ST+AoE damage and CC kit.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Warm Up**: keep Resonance Skill on cooldown outside the burst window (big Resonance Energy gen); use
his Echo right before entering the burst combo (not earlier) so its buff covers the whole Ultimate
window.

**Burst Combo**: Lance of Qingloong only deals damage while its cast animation is actively playing —
dash-canceling or interrupting it exits the state early, securing damage dealt so far at no loss.
Ultimate duration allows 6 Lances + Skill, or 7 Lances; starting and cutting short a 7th Lance
squeezes a small amount of extra damage for minimal rotation-time cost.
Echo → Intro → Forte: Emerald Storm → Heavy: Lance of Qingloong P1 (interrupt ASAP with Skill) →
Resonance Skill → Heavy: Lance P1 → P2 → P3 → Heavy: Lance P1 → P2 → P3 → Resonance Skill → Outro.

**Double Dragon Combo** (advanced, harder timing, more total damage in less time): alternates
Lance-of-Qingloong-then-dodge-cancel to run two Lance-of-Qingloong dragons simultaneously. Risk:
mistimed dodge cancels cut a Lance short, losing hits.
Intro → Forte: Emerald Storm → Heavy: Lance P1 (interrupt with Skill) → Resonance Skill → Heavy: Lance
P1 (Dodge Cancel) ×7 → Resonance Skill → Outro. With perfect play, an 8th Lance is possible (difficult).

**Synergies**: Heavy Attack buffers — Iuno (best overall: buffs + personal damage + Aero synergy),
Phrolova (excellent Dual DPS, some Heavy DMG buffing), Rebecca/Mortefi (free/accessible Heavy DMG
buffers). Unconventional: Ciaccona (buffs persist through swaps — strong in Quickswap Jiyan comps, or
simply the best 3rd-slot alongside Iuno for a casual Mono Aero team), Lynae (alongside Mornye, strong
generalist duo but often better used elsewhere). Universal supports: Shorekeeper/Verina (generalist,
non-specialized buffs), Mornye (niche, only with Rebecca/Lynae for Hack/Tune Rupture), Rover: Aero
(niche, with Ciaccona outside Quickswap, for Aero Erosion synergy).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Ciaccona in 3rd slot only recommended if she's on her Signature Weapon
and on Windward Pilgrimage, with Iuno in team). Erosion team (very strong in Quickswap with
Shorekeeper, can equal his strongest team; Aero Rover recommended for casual players). Lynae Team (only
if Lynae+Mornye have no better home and Iuno is unavailable).

## App Data Comparison (vs. `app/src/data/characters.js` + `jiyan.blocks.js`)

No dump file existed before this pass — created. `SKILL_MULTIPLIERS`/`RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA` values
already matched this source exactly (previously carefully audited against the reference,
including RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA's own detailed per-node breakdown that already confirmed every value —
S1's zeroed fabricated value, S5's split two-effect model, S6's 2-stack-max approximation, all matching
this fresh source word-for-word). `bestWeapon`/`bestEchoes`/`weaponAlts` also already matched exactly.

**Real bugs found and fixed**:
1. `jiyan.outro.discipline` had no `damage.category` at all — the kit text explicitly calls this a
   "Coordinated Attack", which maps directly to this schema's own `coordDmg` category. Fixed.
2. `jiyan.chain.s5-outro-mult` (Outro Discipline DMG Multiplier +120%) was `trigger:{type:'swap-out'}`
   with no `timing.duration` — a NEW VARIANT of the item-12 dead-buff architecture bug: the root cause
   (`resolveHitComposedDps.js`'s `statsAtInstant()` only reads `passiveBlocks`
   [trigger.type==='passive'] and `buffWindows` [duration != null]) applies to ANY non-passive trigger
   type with no duration, not just `'cast'` specifically — every prior instance this session happened
   to use `'cast'`, but `'swap-out'` triggers the identical failure. Fixed via
   `trigger:{type:'passive'}` + `scopedToBlockId:'jiyan.outro.discipline'`.
3. `jiyan.chain.s6` had the more familiar `trigger:{type:'cast',...}` no-duration shape — fixed the
   same way for correctness, though it currently has zero live impact since no
   `jiyan.forte.emerald-storm-finale` damage block exists (Finale is never cast in the real
   `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS`).

3 new/rewritten tests, full suite green (1325/1325).

## Full kit audit (2026-09-08)

Full, independent re-verification of `jiyan.blocks.js` against this dump, `characters.js`
(CHAR_BUFF_TABLE, RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA, SKILL_MULTIPLIERS, CHARACTER_ROTATIONS, full CHARACTER_DATA
entry), and the engine's actual resolver code — per the same rigor applied to Augusta through Jinhsi
this session. Three real bugs found and fixed (one a significant completeness gap, one a real
under-crediting bug, one a re-documented known limitation); everything else re-verified and confirmed
correct.

**Bug 1 — BOTH Inherent Skills and Minor Fortes were entirely missing.** Heavenly Balance ("after Intro
cast, +10% ATK for 15s") and Tempest Taming ("on hit, +12% Crit DMG for 8s") had no block anywhere in
this file, and Minor Fortes (Crit Rate+8%/ATK%+12%) — present for every other character audited this
session (Encore/Galbrena/Hiyuki/Iuno/Jianxin/Jinhsi) — was also absent. Traced upstream: `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE
['Jiyan'].selfBuffs` was empty (`[]`), confirming this was missed in BOTH the legacy and modern engine
paths, not just this one file. Added all three: Minor Fortes as a real passive block, Heavenly Balance
cast-anchored to Intro:Tactical Strike (the same real cast `jiyan.chain.s2`/`jiyan.chain.s5-atk-stack`
already anchor to), and Tempest Taming anchored to the same Intro cast with a sentinel duration (the
real "on hit" trigger is continuously refreshed by the dense ongoing hit rate that follows, same
reasoning as Encore's chain.s1/s6 near-instant-saturation fixes).

**Bug 2 — `chain.s5`'s ATK-stack effect delivered only 1/15 of its real value.** Same under-firing-
anchor bug class already found on Encore's chain.s1/s6 and Iuno's Blessing of the Wan Light this
session: `stacking:'stacking', maxStacks:15, value:3` was anchored to the Intro cast, which fires only
ONCE in the modeled rotation — `activeCountAt()` could never see more than 1 concurrent window, capping
at 1/15 stacks (3% ATK) instead of the real 45%. The block's own OLD note already said this was
"instantly maxed after casting Tactical Strike" — the real mechanic IS a flat 45% from that cast, not a
per-cast ramp. Measured directly: removing the block only moved total damage by ~1.5%, confirming the
under-crediting. Retargeted to a flat 45% (the real, documented instant-max value).

**Also fixed (completeness):** 5 real, sourced SKILL_MULTIPLIERS rows (base Basic ATK combo, standard
Heavy ATK/Windborne Strike/Abyssal Slash, both Mid-air moves, Dodge Counter) had no block anywhere in
this file — every other character audited this session received this "unused base kit for
completeness" pass, but Jiyan was missed. Added all 5 (correctly unused in the modeled rotation, which
enters Qingloong Mode immediately and never lands a base-form input).

**Disclosed, not fixed (real engine limitation):** `chain.s3`'s real trigger is ANY of 4 casts
(Windqueller, Prelude, Finale, OR Intro), but this schema only accepts one cast label per block.
Checked whether a 2nd anchor (Intro) could safely be added, since Intro fires BEFORE the first
Windqueller cast in the modeled rotation (Intro@~1.5s, Windqueller@~6s) — concluded NO: Intro's own 8s
window would still be active when Windqueller's separate block opens its own window, and two
independent blocks would double-apply this crit buff during the overlap (32%/64% instead of the real
single-instance 16%/32%). Kept anchored to Windqueller alone; the real, disclosed consequence (now
documented inline, previously undisclosed) is that Intro's own hit and the first interrupted Lance of
Qingloong cast do not receive this real buff in this model.
