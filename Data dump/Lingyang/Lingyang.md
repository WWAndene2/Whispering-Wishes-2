## Review

- DPS Tier: T3 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA Value), T4 (WW
  Value).
- **Pros**: can fly; unique playstyle found nowhere else; gains the most free stats from his baseline
  S0 kit of any character thanks to his Ultimate boost; a button-mashing combo that's hard to mess up;
  can juggle enemies in the air (excluding elites/bosses), comboing continuously and immobilizing them;
  can ignore some boss/enemy mechanics while airborne.
- **Cons**: falls slightly behind other similarly-roled damage dealers; flying can be disorienting;
  aerial mobility is somewhat limited, making dodging/retargeting/repositioning harder without practice;
  some bosses have unusual hitboxes that make striking them while airborne a challenge, costing damage;
  takes more time to learn than most characters.
- 5★ Glacio Gauntlet DPS, most often a Hyper Carry — the bulk of his damage lands during his Ultimate
  burst window, a considerable chunk of field time incentivizing heavy pre-buffing from Support
  teammates. Very little incentive to swap out mid-window (usually a damage loss), so pair him with
  characters that give him the field time he needs.
- Forte gauge recharges via Furious Punches, Intro, and Ultimate — notably, Intro followed immediately
  by Ultimate can completely fill it, letting players bypass Basic Attacks/Skill for Forte generation
  entirely. At full gauge, Heavy Attack enters Striding Lion (airborne, enhanced kit) for a short
  duration — this is his DPS peak and should be played attentively with all available buffs/support.
- Two Resonance Skills, both with NO cooldown: Ancient Arts (a simple punch, rarely used) and Furious
  Punches (usable only after Basic Attack 3/4/5 or Feral Roars — deals modest damage but generates a
  significant chunk of Forte and chains directly back into Basic Attack 3, enabling fast Basic
  3↔Furious Punches juggling to fill Forte quickly).
- Ultimate: a large strike generating Concerto Energy and a major portion of Forte, +50% Glacio DMG,
  and extends Forte-empowerment duration.
- Field time: must stay for his Ultimate-enhanced Forte's ENTIRE duration for solid burst damage. His
  aerial combat flair grants immunity to some ground attacks but makes others significantly harder to
  dodge — a real drawback requiring practice. Like Jiyan, he can't leave the field mid-rotation without
  a considerable damage loss, best suited to the Hyper Carry archetype.
- Mixed difficulty: easy in concept (build Energy, unload during the Ultimate window) but hard in
  execution (unique aerial navigation, learning dodge timing/hit discernment while airborne) — a major
  contributor to his low playerbase usage.
- As a Main DPS, prefers damage-maximizing Echo/set choices.
- Overall: lower damage than peer 5★ DPS combined with unique, effort-intensive airborne gameplay keeps
  him behind most other endgame DPS in usage.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Combo requirements**: unlike most characters, Lingyang can jump straight into action with no warm-up
phase — the only pre-combo consideration is Echo timing (buff Echoes right before the burst combo,
direct-damage Echoes like Glacial Predator whenever off cooldown).

**Burst Combo**: swap into him with full Energy already available, open with Intro to build part of his
Forte gauge, then Ultimate, then Heavy Attack (Glorious Plunge) to go airborne. While airborne, alternate
a single Basic Attack with a single Resonance Skill (Mountain Roamer) to exploit Diligent Practice —
never chain 2 of the same type back-to-back, or damage is lost. Under ideal execution, 9 independent
attacks fit within the Ultimate's duration (fewer if Stunned or Dodging — not disastrous, just requires
extra Energy-building time from teammates afterward). While his Ultimate is on cooldown or he lacks
Energy, use that time letting the rest of the team recharge Concerto and prep buffs for his next burst.

Sample rotation: Echo (per Echo type's own recommendation) → Intro → Ultimate → Heavy Attack: Glorious
Plunge (enters Forte, goes airborne) → Basic Attack: Feral Gyrate P1 → Skill: Mountain Roamer → Basic:
Feral Gyrate P2 → Skill: Mountain Roamer → Basic: Feral Gyrate P1 → Skill: Mountain Roamer → Basic:
Feral Gyrate P2 → Skill: Mountain Roamer → Basic: Feral Gyrate P1 → Basic: Stormy Kicks → Mid-Air
Attack: Tail Strike → Outro.

**Synergies**: Lynae/Zhezhi (his best partners — Lynae has the strongest generalist buffs in the game
and forms a particularly strong core with Mornye for extra Tune Break damage and one of the strongest
buff arrays he can access; Zhezhi is also very strong, dealing good off-field personal damage via
Coordinated Attacks plus a great 20% Glacio/25% Skill DMG Outro he heavily appreciates). Sanhua (one of
the fastest rotations in the game, 38% Basic Attack DMG Amp on Outro buffs him hugely — often used if
Zhezhi is taken by another team or unowned, since Sanhua is free). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist
Healing Supports slotting into any team — Verina: fast rotation, team-wide ATK%/15% DMG Amp;
Shorekeeper: 12.5% Crit Rate/25% Crit DMG/25% ATK/15% DMG Amp).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Mornye is only best here when paired WITH Lynae specifically), F2P Team —
exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this extraction; names/conditions are directly from the
source text.
