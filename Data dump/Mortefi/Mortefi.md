## Review

- DPS Tier (Hybrid): T1.5 (Tower of Adversity), T2 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier: T1.5 / T2.
- **Pros**: incredibly fast Concerto rotation (fast Outro access); incredible synergy with Heavy
  Attackers (boosts them and himself); good personal damage given his buff kit; strong Sequences;
  easy to play at any skill level; strong enough as a Hybrid even outside the Heavy Attacker niche.
- **Cons**: long Ultimate cooldown (20s vs. the buff's own 10s duration — plan rotations around this);
  S0 feels weak compared to his overpowered Sequences.
- 4★ Fusion Pistol Hybrid, shines as a Support for Heavy-Attack-focused teammates: Ultimate synergizes
  with Heavy Attacks, Outro amplifies them a further 38%.
- Forte fills via Basic Attack/Skill/Intro; at full, Skill becomes Fury Fugue — a great Concerto/
  Resonance Energy generator with AoE Fusion DMG, no cooldown, doesn't interfere with normal Skill use
  (can chain both quickly). Filled fastest via Basic Attacks right after Skill.
- Skill: solid Concerto/Resonance Energy + Fusion DMG to primary target.
- Ultimate: Fusion DMG in front + generates Concerto + buffs the team with Burning Rhapsody — Mortefi
  launches a Coordinated Attack whenever an ally hits an enemy (up to once per 0.35s); Heavy Attack
  hits trigger 2 Coordinated Attacks instead of 1, doubling potential output — Heavy Attackers are his
  ideal partners.
- Minimal field-time requirement thanks to one of the fastest/easiest Concerto rotations in the game;
  forgiving even when misplayed.
- True calling is Support via Outro: prefers 5P Moonlit Clouds + Impermanence Heron Echo (+12% DMG,
  +22% ATK stacked on top of his 38% Heavy ATK Amp) for a massive buff to the incoming character.
- One of the top Hybrid characters in endgame for Heavy-Attack-centered teams; also valuable purely for
  his rotation speed and Moonlit Clouds/Impermanence Heron synergy even outside that niche.

## Build

**Best Weapon**: Static Mist (R1, listed first/recommended) — ER+12.8%; Outro cast → +10% ATK to the
switched-in Resonator for 14s, stacks ×1. 100% (listed as the top pick for its QOL/support value, even
though the raw calc % below shows a higher number for The Last Dance — matches the source's own stated

**Best Echo Set**: Moonlit Clouds — 2pc: ER+10%; 5pc: Outro cast → +22.5% ATK to next Resonator for
15s. Main Echo: Impermanence Heron (supportive — trades personal damage for a further +12% DMG to the
next Resonator on Outro-swap-cancel, plus a big Resonance Energy refund). Alt: Empyrean Anthem — 2pc:
ER+10%; 5pc: +80% Coordinated Attack DMG, Coordinated Attack crit → +20% ATK to on-field Resonator for
4s. Better than Moonlit Clouds ONLY with endgame Echo investment (9-10 Crit substats) AND a 5★ weapon;
otherwise Moonlit Clouds wins. Main Echo: Hecate (+40% unconditional Coordinated Attack DMG).

**Substats priority**: Energy Regen (until satisfied) > Crit Rate = Crit DMG > ATK% > ATK.

**Endgame stat targets (Lv.90)**: HP 15000+, DEF 1100+, ATK 1900-2200+, Crit Rate 60-80%, Crit DMG
215-260%+, Energy Regen 115-130%+ (at S2, ~120% in most teams, 113% with Augusta due to her rotation
extension; without S2, +~11% more needed), Fusion DMG Bonus 40-70%+.

**Skill priority**: Liberation > Forte Circuit > Basic Attack > Skill > Intro.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Rotation notes**: always use Ultimate + Outro together right before swapping to another character
(ideally a Heavy Attacker) — casting the Ultimate too early wastes damage/buffing potential (especially
at S6). Passionate Variation opens a 5s window where Basic Attacks grant extra Annoyance — use it to
refill Fury Fugue fast. Ultimate is always used at rotation's end and generates ~20 Concerto (a bit
under a quarter of the gauge) — plan to cast it when ~20 Concerto short.

**Rotation**: Intro → Skill: Passionate Variation → Skill: Fury Fugue → Basic P1 → P2 → P3 → P4 →
Skill: Fury Fugue → Ultimate → Echo (Impermanence Heron swap-cancel).

**Synergies**: Augusta (best F2P-accessible option for her — massive 38% Heavy DMG Amp on an easily-
accessed Outro, plus solid off-field Marcato damage; also gets ATK% from Static Mist and S6).
Galbrena (one of her best partners — 38% Heavy DMG Amp on one of her two main damage types, plus ATK%/
DMG% via Moonlit Clouds + Static Mist; great with Lupa in Mono Fusion). Jiyan (tailor-made partner —
amplifies his main damage type, Heavy ATK DMG, by 38% on Outro, plus lots of ATK% buffs). Phoebe (good
secondary for Absolution Phoebe with Rejuvenating Glow Spectro Rover — Heavy ATK Amp has diminishing
returns for her, so his value there is mostly S6/Static Mist ATK%; contested but strong pairing). Zani
(substitute for Phoebe's buffing, but applies no Spectro Frazzle — must be paired with Spectro Rover to
compensate; works fine for F2P clears). Verina/Shorekeeper (easy generalist healing supports —
Shorekeeper adds Crit Rate/Crit DMG/ATK%/DMG Amp on top).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, Galbrena Mono Fusion Team, Alternative Heavy Attack Teams, Phoebe Team
(Spectro Rover on Rejuvenating Glow), Zani Team (Spectro Rover on Rejuvenating Glow) — exact roster
icons weren't machine-readable in this extraction; inferred from the Synergies section text.

(`chain.s1-bonus-marcato` and `chain.s5-bonus-marcato`) — matching the Augusta S3/Brant S3&S6
over-crediting fix pattern. Currently latent (his real `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS` casts nothing else within
the post-Liberation window), but a real correctness fix regardless.

the source's own ranking — corrected.

## Full kit audit (2026-09-09)

Independent re-audit (not trusting the prior 2026-09-01/09-04 passes' own claims of completeness, per
standing audit instruction) of `engine/characterBlocks/mortefi.blocks.js` against this dump.

**1 real bug found and fixed**: S5's real kit text names TWO trigger casts — "Passionate Variation or
Fury Fugue hits → fires 4 Marcato hits at -50% DMG" (line 76 above), also confirmed by
`RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA`'s own comment ("S5 Funerary Quartet — Skill/Fury Fugue hits fire 4 bonus
Marcato hits") — but only the Fury Fugue anchor was ever wired into a block; the Passionate Variation
cast (a real, separate step that always fires before either Fury Fugue cast in the modeled rotation)
never got its own proc. Added `mortefi.chain.s5-bonus-marcato-skill`, anchored to
`Skill:Passionate Variation`, same value/shape as the existing Fury-Fugue-anchored block. Also added
it to `chain.s3`'s own Crit DMG scoping list for forward correctness (currently inert in the modeled
rotation since Passionate Variation fires before Burning Rhapsody starts, but the underlying mechanic
doesn't distinguish which of S5's two real Marcato sources triggered it).

**Verification**: measured directly at Sequence 5 — the new proc adds real, non-zero damage
(+882.66 in the test enemy-config). Parity-golden fixtures unaffected (the golden snapshot runs at
Sequence 0, where S5 doesn't apply). 2 new positive-verification tests added to
`triggerEngine-mortefi.test.js`. Full test suite re-run and green (1871/1871).

**2 Inherent Skills examined, deliberately left unmodeled (documented, not silently dropped)**:
- **Harmonic Control** ("after Passionate Variation cast, Draconic Hellfire damage +25% for 8s") —
  "Draconic Hellfire" names no move anywhere in Mortefi's own kit text, and no other character's dump
  in this project contains that string either. This reads as a scraping/template artifact in the
  source page rather than a real mechanic — modeling it would require guessing which real move it's
  actually supposed to name, which this project's "zero, don't guess" rule forbids. Flagged for a
  cleaner source re-check rather than modeled or silently ignored.
- **Rhythmic Vibrato** ("during Burning Rhapsody, each Marcato hit → next Marcato's damage +1.5%,
  stacking ×50") — a real, sourced, but genuinely complex escalating per-proc ramp this schema has no
  primitive for (no mechanism exists anywhere in this codebase for "a bonus that escalates per proc of
  a specific block within one duration window"). The stated 50-stack cap is itself unreachable given
  Burning Rhapsody's own hard 0.35s-per-proc rate cap over its 10s duration (max ~28 real procs, not
  50). Flagged as a known, real, unrepresentable-in-schema gap — same treatment as Mornye's own S5
  Particle Jet DMG Multiplier gap — rather than force-fit into an undefensible approximation.

**Everything else re-verified this pass, found already correct**: S1/S2/S3/S4/S6's mechanics and
scoping, the base-kit Burning Rhapsody Marcato proc-count derivation, the Outro pairing against
`CHAR_BUFF_TABLE`, `dmgFocus`, `weaponAlts`, and `SKILL_MULTIPLIERS`/`CHARACTER_ROTATIONS`/
`CHARACTER_DATA` entries generally.
