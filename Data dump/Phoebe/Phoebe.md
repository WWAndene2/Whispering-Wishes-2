## Review

- DPS Tier: T1.5 (Tower of Adversity), T3 (Whimpering Wastes). Hybrid Tier: T2 / T3. Value Tier (DPS):
  T1.5 / T3. Value Tier (Hybrid): T3 / T3.
- **Pros**: playable as DPS (Absolution) or Hybrid (Confession); Absolution has wide, consistent,
  easily-exploitable AoE damage + very F2P-friendly team options; Confession has a short rotation with
  huge Spectro Frazzle-related buffs; arguably the most F2P-friendly limited 5★ in the game.
- **Cons**: Absolution needs a Spectro Frazzle applier teammate (restricts teambuilding, weak in
  multiwave content, minimal Outro-buff benefit since her own Heavy Attacks already have massive DMG
  Amp); Confession deals subpar damage on its own and is currently only useful for Zani.
- Dual-mode: Absolution (Main DPS) vs. Confession (Hybrid Frazzle-support, like Sanhua/Mortefi —
  applies the most Frazzle of any character, can Spectro RES shred).
- Prayer (cap 120, 5/s, ~24s to fill) gates mode entry: full-Prayer Heavy Attack → Absolution Litany;
  full-Prayer held Skill → Utter Confession. Both refill Divine Voice (cap 60) fully, fueling Starflash
  (Absolution: 15 cost, 4 casts/bar, +256% Frazzle-target Amp; Confession: 30 cost, 2 casts/bar, +5
  Frazzle stacks/cast).
- Basic Attacks mostly ignorable outside the Ring of Mirrors (enhanced Skill zone, 30s): inside it,
  Basic Attack deals ~double via Chamuel's Star, same in both modes.
- Ultimate: +255% multiplier in Absolution / applies 8 Frazzle stacks in Confession. Outro: +255%
  multiplier in Absolution / grants Silent Prayer (-10% Spectro RES, +100% Frazzle DMG Amp) in
  Confession.
- Typical rotation: Skill (Ring of Mirrors) → enter a mode → Ultimate (can animation-cancel the mode
  entry cast) → enter the Ring → Basic ×3 + Starflash (repeated 2-4x by mode) → Outro.
- Weaknesses: Absolution needs Frazzle re-applied to any newly-spawned enemies (bad for Whimpering
  Wastes); Confession's only current Main DPS partner is Zani.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Standard Absolution DPS Rotation**: Intro → Skill: Mirror Summon → Skill: Mirror Teleport (optional)
→ Forte: Absolution Litany → Ultimate → Basic: Chamuel's Star P1-3 → Forte: Starflash (×4 cycles) →
Outro.

**Standard Confession DPS Rotation** (only optimal with Zani): Intro → Skill: Mirror Summon → Mirror
Teleport (optional) → Forte: Utter Confession → Ultimate → Basic: Chamuel's Star P1-3 → Forte: Starflash
(×2 cycles) → Outro.

**Optimized tricks** (both modes): Dash Cancel during Chamuel's Star P3 (no damage cost, saves time);
Swap Cancel during Starflash (weave in teammates at no field-time cost); cancel Absolution Litany's/
Utter Confession's animation with the Ultimate immediately after (retains all damage, saves time).

**Synergies**: Zani (Confession Phoebe's only current synergy — massive Frazzle DMG buff via Outro +
Spectro RES Shred, one of the meta's top teams). Frazzle appliers: Rover: Spectro (single-wave), Ciaccona
(multi-wave), Chisa (quickswap secondary damage + buffs). Best 2nd-slot buffers for DPS Phoebe: Lynae
(strongest generalist, her best team), Rebecca (close 2nd — ramp-up Heavy DMG buff + personal damage),
Phrolova (more damage-oriented, Heavy ATK DMG Amp Outro + personal damage/CC at moderate field time).
Additional buffers: Mortefi/Iuno (Heavy ATK DMG Amp — low marginal value since she's already saturated
there), Sanhua (F2P Basic ATK buffer, equivalent profile to Mortefi).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Absolution), Alternative Team (Absolution — Ciaccona is the best/only
Whimpering Wastes partner, paired there with Lynae/Shorekeeper/Chisa in Quickswap), Best Team
(Confession — Spectro Rover strongest via Quickswap, Shorekeeper easiest for most players), Expert
Quickswap (Absolution, high-skill only), Triple Spectro Team (Absolution, Spectro Rover on Moonlit

## Full kit audit (2026-09-09)

Independent re-audit (not trusting either prior pass's own claims of completeness, per standing audit
instruction) of `engine/characterBlocks/phoebe.blocks.js` and `characters.js`'s `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS`
against this dump — despite this being the character with the most documented prior audit depth in
the whole roster (2 full passes, 13 prior fixes).

**2 real bugs found and fixed, both significant**:

1. **4x undercount on Starflash/Chamuel's Star** — `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS['Phoebe']`'s OWN note already
   said "Repeat the '3 Basics into Starflash' pattern exactly 4 times (60/15 Divine Voice)" (matching
   this dump's own text, line 75 above), but only 1 of the 4 real cycles was ever encoded as array
   steps. Starflash is her single biggest real damage bucket ("Heavy 43.8%" per this dump's own Damage
   Profile), so this silently discarded roughly 3/4 of her real Heavy Attack damage and Basic ATK
   damage (Chamuel's Star shares the same cycle). Fixed by adding the 3 missing repetitions. Measured:
   total damage at Sequence 0 rose from 75,743 to 125,508 (+66%) — Heavy-category share after the fix
   (Starflash + Absolution Litany, 57,870 / 125,508 = 46.1%) now closely matches the dump's own 43.8%,
   strongly confirming the fix's correctness (before the fix, Heavy share was badly understated).
2. **S6's "free extra Starflash" proc missing its own multipliers** — `phoebe.chain.s6-free-starflash`
   is narratively the same move as Starflash (per its own note, reusing Starflash's raw %ATK), but
   neither `chain.s3`'s own +91% DMG Multiplier nor the base-kit `+256%` Frazzle-target Amp
   (`phoebe.kit.starflash-frazzle-amp`) had it in their `scopedToBlockId` lists — any Sequence-6 player
   would see this "same move" proc dealing roughly 1/7th of what a real Starflash instance deals.
   Fixed by adding `phoebe.chain.s6-free-starflash` to both scoping lists. Measured: the free proc's
   damage rose from ~4241 to ~27,363 (vs. a real Starflash instance's ~29,954 at the same Sequence,
   now in the right order of magnitude — the small remaining gap is a legitimate timing-window
   difference, not a bug).

**Verification**: both fixes measured directly via a temporary script (deleted after use).

despite total damage rising 66%, since the added real casts also add real rotation TIME; the two
