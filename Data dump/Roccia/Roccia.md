## Review

**Ratings**: Hybrid — T3 (Tower of Adversity), T3 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA
Value), T3 (WW Value, not separately confirmed in this extraction).

**Pros**: her grouping utility effectively counts as an extra Echo Skill cast for the team; best JP VA
in the cast, plus a cute pet (per the source's own framing).

**Cons**: outclassed in every team she's built for — Sanhua/Lynae outclass her as Camellya's buffer,
Qiuyuan/Lucilla/Shorekeeper outclass her in Phrolova teams; easily replaced by the free 4★s Danjin and
Sanhua; low Swap Cancel headroom due to an interruptible mid-air chain (similar to Qiuyuan); her
Signature is "skippable" in the sense that it's not required to function, but she needs it just to beat
the 4★ alternatives; very inconsistent without S1.

**Summary**: the source is explicit and repeated that Roccia is one of the least valuable limited 5★s
in the game. As Camellya's buffer she's a sidegrade to the fully free Sanhua (whose buffs are barely
worse, and Camellya doesn't need the grouping since her own kit already has wide AoE). As the third
slot in a Phrolova+Cantarella trio, she's barely better than Danjin, worse than Shorekeeper, and well
behind Qiuyuan/Lucilla. Even with her Signature equipped her personal damage barely clears her 4★
competition. "Destined to fail... here's to hoping for a Roccia kit buff, because she needs one."

## Gameplay and Teams

**Standard Rotation**: Intro → Basic ATK (Stage 4, skipping straight there) → Skill: Acrobatic Trick →
Forte: Real Fantasy 1-3 → Liberation: Commedia Improvviso! (cast the instant the 3rd bounce lands, to
cancel its landing recovery) → Echo (Swap Cancel) → Outro. Echo may be cast before Intro instead, if
the team has proper Swap Cancel windows.

**S1+ Hybrid Rotation**: skips Basic ATK Stage 4 (S1's extra Imagination from Acrobatic Trick covers
the gap).

**S6 Main DPS Rotation**: uses Reality Recreation to loop Real Fantasy bounces indefinitely rather than
ending the rotation after 3.

**Synergies**: Camellya (Outro's Havoc+Basic ATK DMG Amp lines up naturally with Camellya's own damage
types — "Camellya's best in slot buffer, no doubt, despite being very competitive with Sanhua").
Phrolova (one of the best third-slot teammates to the Phrolova-Cantarella trio, as the best recipient
of Phrolova's Havoc+Heavy Attack Outro Amp, keeping the trio's damage upkept). Rover: Havoc ("easily
Havoc Rover's best buffer" — her Outro Amp and grouping pair well with Rover's small-AoE big-hit
kit). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing Supports that can slot into any of her teams).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, Camellya Team, Havoc Rover Team — exact roster icons weren't
machine-readable in this extraction; team names are directly from the source text, and the Camellya/
Phrolova team compositions are cross-referenced from the Synergies section text itself (Camellya+
Shorekeeper, Phrolova+Cantarella).
