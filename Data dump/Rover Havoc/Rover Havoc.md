## Review

- DPS Tier: T3 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T3 (ToA Value), T4 (WW
  Value).
- **Pros**: usable effectively as both Main DPS and burst Dual DPS; versatile team options; simple
  playstyle with easy rotations; damage can be frontloaded to finish enemies quickly (low-cooldown
  Ultimate + a signature Echo nuke, both very high damage); a Sword user with lots of viable, commonly
  obtained 5★ options; free, and Sequences are free too — easy to build.
- **Cons**: one of the longest Main DPS on-field-time requirements in the game; falls behind limited
  options as a Dual DPS due to long Forte buildup; low AoE potential outside big cooldown-gated nukes.
- A free 5★ Havoc DPS, unlocked after completing Act I Chapter 6. Unlike most Main DPS with their
  combos locked behind Ultimate, they use Forte (Umbra) to access a whole enhanced stance — though the
  Ultimate still hits like a freight train. Their kit isn't centered on one damage type or ability —
  Skill, Ultimate, and Forte Circuit ALL contribute meaningfully, but the central mechanic is the "Dark
  Surge" state, accessed by accumulating 100 Umbra via Intro, Resonance Skill, and Basic Attacks.
- At full Umbra, "Devastation" enters Dark Surge and shreds enemy Havoc RES by 10% for 20s. In this
  state, Forte depletes continuously (even off-field) until empty; during it they gain: increased Havoc
  DMG Bonus, extra Resonance Energy recovery, enhanced Basic Attacks that heal on the final hit, +25%
  Crit Rate, and an instantly-refreshed, enhanced, slightly-Dark-Surge-extending Resonance Skill.
- Fastest/most effective Dark Surge access: cast Resonance Skill before Support/Hybrid rotations
  (cooldown permitting), then Intro + a 2nd Resonance Skill cast when entering the field fully buffed —
  this is the method used in all optimized rotations. Skipping this forces a full Basic Attack string
  off Dark Surge entry, which is suboptimal.
- In Dark Surge, casting Intro restores the Dark Surge gauge — paired with the enhanced Skill cast, this
  lets them use TWO enhanced Skills per Dark Surge, extending it further (useful for burst-focused Dual
  DPS play). With just one enhanced Skill, the duration barely allows 3 full Basic Attack chains
  uninterrupted (useful for standard Main DPS rotations).
- Unique 4-cost signature Echo: Dreamless. Used within 5s of Ultimate landing → +50% DMG — a massive
  360° AoE burst similar in power to Yinlin's Ultimate, the highest-damaging Echo skill in the game.
  Instantly Swap-Cancellable with zero downside — normally used outside the burst window itself, giving
  "two Ultimates in one." A huge reason their damage is strong regardless of Main or Dual DPS role.
- Ultimate: an extremely simple, insanely high-damage cleave nuke at just 125 Energy / 16s cooldown —
  unheard-of efficiency for a 1520% multiplier at Lv.10 (comparable abilities usually cost 150 Energy
  with longer cooldowns). Thanks to Dark Surge's extra Energy generation, they need ZERO Energy Regen
  investment unless played as a burst-focused Dual DPS — another huge selling point.
- These quick bursts (Ultimate, Echo, and even single-target Resonance Skills, which refresh instantly
  on Dark Surge entry) make them strong as a Dual DPS if two DPS cooldown tracks can be managed at once
  — difficult, but rewarding when mastered.
- Their biggest flaw doubles as a big source of their damage: Dark Surge Basic Attacks are relatively
  slow to execute for only average-tier multipliers versus top DPS characters. Less problematic in Dual
  DPS, but there they still trail premium options like Changli/Brant since Rover: Havoc's Outro is a
  DAMAGE Outro, not a BUFFING one, and swap windows (final Basic Attack, Skills, Echo) are either slow
  to reach or cooldown-gated — not ideal for quickswap.
- As a Main DPS, damage potential is comparable to Encore/Jiyan/Xiangli Yao, largely thanks to S6's
  massive boost and strong frontloading via Skill/Echo/Ultimate. Notable synergy: Danjin, who can even
  be played as a Dual DPS partner for experienced players — a free 4★+free 5★ pairing that's one of the
  best teams in the game for both beginners and experts. Roccia (2.0) was a major AoE-nuke and Main-DPS
  upgrade thanks to her grouping and higher buffing.
- Summary: often overlooked for being free, but remains a strong Main DPS carry AND a Dual DPS burst
  option, sitting comfortably in the high tiers with flexible team options.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Warm-up**: top priority is using Resonance Skill on cooldown during downtime between Umbra burst
windows; also land Basic Attacks when possible to maintain 5P Havoc Eclipse's stacking buff. His
rotation is flexible — short or long, with real tradeoffs either way.

**Short Burst Combo** (max damage in minimum time, ignores Basic Attacks entirely — best for quickswap
teams with another field-time-hungry DPS): Intro → Skill → Heavy ATK: Devastation → Skill: Umbra:
Lifetaker (optionally Swap-Cancel) → Ultimate → Echo (Dreamless Swap Cancel) → Outro.

**Medium Burst Combo** (higher total damage over a longer duration — for teams where he's the sole DPS
with 2 Supports): Intro → Skill → Heavy ATK: Devastation → Umbra: Basic P1-5 (×2 full passes) → Skill:
Umbra: Lifetaker (optionally Swap Cancel) → Ultimate → Echo (Dreamless Swap Cancel) → Outro.

**Long Burst Combo** (for players with excellent enemy awareness who can spam Basics without dodging —
fits a full 3 Basic Attack sequences in Umbra state, longest field time, slightly higher total damage;
some abilities may fall outside Umbra depending on the fight, adding risk): Intro → Skill → Heavy ATK:
Devastation → Umbra: Basic P1-5 → Skill: Umbra: Lifetaker (optionally Swap Cancel) → Umbra: Basic P1-5 →
Ultimate → Umbra: Basic P1-5 → Echo (Dreamless Swap Cancel) → Outro.

**Synergies**: Roccia (his best buffer — an amazing 20% Havoc + 25% Basic Attack DMG Amplify on Outro,
plus good personal damage and excellent grouping he benefits from on his Ultimate/Echo — an extremely
natural pair). Phrolova (doesn't buff him or benefit much from his Outro, since he doesn't deal much
Heavy Attack damage, but fills lots of field time with great damage + Resonance Energy generation — one
of the highest-damage partners for Phrolova teams as an alternative option). Danjin (surprisingly his
2nd-best buffer after Roccia, very close in strength — pair with short, low-damage rotations for her
23% Havoc DMG Amplify on Outro, which he benefits from thanks to having more than one damage type — an
extremely strong F2P pair). Sanhua (3rd-best option if you don't want to invest in Danjin but own her —
very short rotation, 38% Basic ATK DMG Amplify on Outro he uses well). Cantarella (good alternative —
20% Havoc + 25% Skill DMG Amplify on Outro, plus good personal damage and extra heals; less recommended
since Danjin/Sanhua rotate faster and don't share her Energy Regen requirements — still competitive if
you want the heals or like the character). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing Supports slotting
into any team — Verina: fast rotation, team-wide ATK%/15% DMG Amp; Shorekeeper: 12.5% Crit Rate/25%
Crit DMG/25% ATK/15% DMG Amp).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, Phrolova Team (team order depends on the 3rd teammate — with Danjin,
Danjin buffs Rover: Havoc and Phrolova buffs Danjin; with Cantarella, Cantarella buffs Phrolova and
Phrolova buffs Rover: Havoc), F2P Team (Danjin performs slightly stronger than Sanhua, but Sanhua works
fine without investing in Danjin), Cantarella Alternative (only worth it for the extra heals — the
other teams outperform it otherwise) — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this extraction;
team names/conditions are directly from the source text.
