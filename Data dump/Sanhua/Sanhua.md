## Review

- DPS Tier (Hybrid): T2 (Tower of Adversity), T3 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T1.5 (ToA
  Value), T3 (WW Value).
- **Pros**: one of, if not the fastest, Concerto rotations in the game; great burst damage combining
  Ultimate/Intro/Skill with a Forte Burst; absurd Concerto gain from detonating ice constructs; low 100
  Energy cost keeps her burst combo consistently repeatable; free — extra copies grant Sequences; many
  viable rotations, all strong enough to pick freely.
- **Cons**: the combo is so fast that inputting everything correctly and on time can be overwhelming;
  requires successfully timing her Forte minigame every single rotation to complete it.
- Free 4★ Glacio Sword Hybrid dealing great damage for absurdly low field time. Also a strong Support
  thanks to her Outro's 38% Basic Attack Amplify and easy access to one of the best supportive Echo
  sets in the game — combined, one of the strongest and most popular Hybrids, with room to scale
  further via Sequences.
- Forte mechanic: a charge-and-release minigame on Heavy Attack — a pulsing indicator on her Gauge;
  releasing while the cursor falls in the "Frostbite" (blue) area triggers Detonate, exploding all
  active ice glaciers/prisms/thorns for Glacio DMG plus large Concerto/Resonance Energy gains. The blue
  area is normally tiny and hard to hit, but expands significantly after Basic Attack 5, Intro Skill,
  Resonance Skill, or Ultimate.
- Ultimate deals Glacio DMG in front, generates solid Concerto, and spawns a large Glacier (detonable
  via Forte for even more Concerto + Resonance Energy). Skill spawns a small Ice Prism that can coexist
  with the Ultimate's Glacier, letting both stack for a single combined detonation.
- Requires minimal field time thanks to the fastest Concerto rotation in the game, though tricky to
  execute perfectly — the insanely fast input pace plus correct Forte-minigame timing is the main
  difficulty.
- True calling is Support via Outro — best paired with 5P Moonlit Clouds + Impermanence Heron Echo,
  stacking an additional 12% DMG + 22% ATK on top of the 38% Basic Attack Amplify for a massive buff on
  the incoming character.
- Currently one of the top Hybrid characters in endgame (alongside Mortefi), especially for Basic-Attack
  -focused teams/burst rotations, but sees strong use even outside that niche thanks to her rotation
  speed and Moonlit Clouds/Impermanence Heron synergy.

## Gameplay and Teams

Core focus: deploy her 3 Forte Ice constructs (1 each from Intro/Skill/Ultimate), shatter all 3 with a
Heavy Attack Detonate before they expire (each construct detonated generates 15 Concerto — 45 total,
not to be missed), and time her Outro to land the 38% Basic Attack Amplify on the intended damage dealer.
**Echo usage**: activate Impermanence Heron near the end of her rotation, right before her Outro, and
Swap Cancel it.

**Heavy Attack Concerto Rotation** (the more commonly played variant): Intro → Ultimate → Skill → Heavy
Attack: Detonate → Heavy Attack: Detonate → Outro.

**S5+ Heavy Attack Concerto Rotation** (shortened once Ice Thorn/Prism/Glacier interactions from S5+
grant extra Concerto): Intro → Ultimate → Skill → Heavy Attack: Detonate → Echo (Impermanence Heron
Switch Cancel) → Outro.

**Basic Attack Concerto Rotation**: Intro → Basic ATK P3 → P4 → P5 → Ultimate → Skill → Heavy Attack:
Detonate → Outro.

**Synergies**: Camellya (a very close second to Roccia in Camellya teams — so close there's little
incentive to pull Roccia; short field time + massive 38% Basic ATK Amp is all Camellya teams need from
her). Encore ("by far Encore's best teammate, period" — over any quickswap or limited 5★ options; the
pair easily clears any content in the game, an amazing F2P duo). Phoebe (best teammate for casual
players running Rejuvenating Glow Spectro Rover Phoebe — fast-accessed 38% Basic ATK Amp on Outro, plus
Moonlit Clouds buffs and a 20% ATK S6 buff on a super-fast rotation make Sanhua+Phoebe+Spectro Rover
"the best casual Phoebe trio"). Brant (a very natural Hypercarry-Brant partner, since he deals almost
exclusively Basic ATK DMG — strong results, though not as strong as Brant's Dual DPS teams). Rover:
Havoc (outclassed by Roccia on the 5★ side and Danjin on the 4★ side in Havoc Rover teams, since Sanhua's
buff is too narrowly Basic-Attack-focused for Havoc Rover's spread damage types — still a good option
if you don't want to invest in either alternative). Carlotta (outclassed by Zhezhi on the 5★ side and
Yangyang on the 4★ side — buffs Carlotta very little and deals negligible damage herself in that team —
comparable enough to use if you lack both alternatives). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing
Supports slotting into any team — Verina: fast rotation, team-wide ATK%/15% DMG Amp; Shorekeeper:
12.5% Crit Rate/25% Crit DMG/25% ATK/15% DMG Amp).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, Encore Team, Brant Hypercarry Team, Budget Havoc Rover Team, Carlotta
Team — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this extraction; team names are directly from the
source text and correspond to the named Synergies above.
