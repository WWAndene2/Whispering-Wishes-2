## Review

**DPS tier**: **T0** (Tower of Adversity, standard) / **T0.5** (Whimpering Wastes, standard) / **T0** (Tower of Adversity, Value) / **T1** (Whimpering Wastes, Value) — labeled **Support**.

**Pros**
- Best Support for a lot of Negative Status characters — a currently very strong meta archetype.
- Easy rotation, almost no complexity.
- Built-in grouping for multi-wave content.
- F2P-friendly, fully operational at S0R0 — Variation is a fine replacement for her Signature.

**Cons**
- Full buffs only activate under the specific condition of direct Negative Status consumption — limits team flexibility (S1 tries to help but doesn't much in practice).
- Very low/nonexistent quickswap headroom — needs an Intro for a quick rotation start, wants to stay on field for full attack chains, some buffs expire on swap and take time to re-trigger.
- Low sequence value in general — one of the lowest in the modern era, basically no room for vertical investment.

**Key mechanics**
- Two stances: **Zephyr** (default, only really used in the Opener) and **Drizzle** (entered via Intro directly, or via full Zephyr Forte → Skill). Each stance has its own moves and Forte gauge.
- Zephyr's Forte (Cloud Breath) at full lets her cast a special Skill (Awakening Spring) to switch into Drizzle — useful without an Intro. Drizzle's Forte (Floral Epistle) is consumed on Outro to unlock her full buffs.
- Typical rotation: enter Drizzle ASAP (Zephyr Basics → Skill, or Intro directly), fill Floral Epistle, deploy buffs via Outro. Liberation is also cast every rotation — it raises the max stack cap of all Negative Statuses except Havoc Bane by 3 (similar to Chisa), and consuming Havoc Bane grants a small DEF Ignore/Havoc RES Shred boost.
- Buffing profile (concentrated in Outro + Liberation, plus Echo Set/Signature): +3 max stack cap for any Negative Status but Havoc Bane; Havoc Bane consumers get +6% DEF Ignore/+12% Havoc RES Shred; +12% general DMG Bonus and +25% general DMG Amplification via Outro; +50% ATK for Negative-Status-consuming Hypercarries via Outro (expires on swap); 5 total Glacio Chafe applications per rotation via her kit; +25% ATK (+20% more via Echo Set/Signature if owned).
- Currently the only 2 characters able to fully leverage her buffs: Hiyuki and Yangyang: Xuanling.

**Meta position**: restrictive buffer requiring Negative-Status-consuming teammates (ideally a Hypercarry DPS) to use her buffs fully — only the 50% ATK component is strictly Negative-Status-gated, and even that can be forfeited in her one other good team (Aemeath Fusion Burst). Best-in-slot for Hiyuki (Glacio Chafe synergy) and Yangyang: Xuanling (Havoc Bane synergy) — 2 of the strongest current meta teams — and a solid Chisa replacement in those + a viable Aemeath Fusion Burst alternative.

## Build

### Best Weapons
| Weapon | Rank |
|---|---|
| Firstlight's Herald (R1, signature) | 1st |
| Stellar Symphony (R1, Shorekeeper's signature) | 2nd |
| Variation (R5, 4★) | 3rd (best cost-efficiency) |
| Call of the Abyss (R5, 4★) | 4th (last resort) |

**Signature (Firstlight's Herald)**: +12% Max HP; Liberation restores 8 Concerto Energy (once per 20s); inflicting Glacio Chafe grants Snow Taint (6s); healing grants Ripples (6s); doing both while on-field makes the next Outro grant both effects for 6s; holding both Snow Taint and Ripples grants all nearby team members +20% ATK (same-name effects don't stack). Best-in-slot — team-wide 20% ATK buff plus the Energy Regen she needs for her full buffs.
**Stellar Symphony**: provides enough Energy Regen to max her passives, but otherwise redundant (her team-wide ATK passive there doesn't activate on Suisui).
**Variation**: best cost-efficiency overall — Concerto + Energy Regen for baseline operation at minimal team damage loss.
**Call of the Abyss**: last resort — same Energy Regen with Healing Bonus instead of Concerto Energy, slows her rotation significantly but acceptable as a stopgap.

### Best Echo Sets

**1) Song of Feathered Trace** — 2pc: +10% Energy Regen. 5pc: inflicting Havoc Bane grants Xuanling's Feather (+20% Crit Rate, +35% Heavy Attack DMG Bonus, 15s); inflicting Glacio Chafe grants Chongming's Feather (+0.1% team ATK per 1% of the Resonator's Energy Regen, up to +25%, 10s). Lets Suisui buff the whole team's ATK by +25% constantly via her Glacio Chafe application and Energy Regen scaling — best overall set, best buff uptime.
Main Echo: **Forbidden Bastion** — +10% Healing Bonus in main slot, simple Summon Echo with minor damage/healing.

**2) Rejuvenating Glow** — 2pc: +10% Healing. 5pc: healing allies grants the whole team +15% ATK for 30s. Easy 100% uptime, good party-wide damage increase.
Main Echo: **Fallacy of No Return** — gives Rejuvenating Glow the same total buff count as Song of Feathered Trace on cast (+10% team ATK, +10% Energy Regen to wearer); summon before Outro for roughly Signature-set-equivalent effects, though lower uptime in longer rotations makes it situationally (slightly) worse.

**Best Echo Stats**: 4-cost Crit DMG · 3-cost Energy Regen · 3-cost Energy Regen / HP% (HP% main-stat on the 3-cost slot only realistic with a 5★ high-ER weapon + high substat rolls) · 1-cost HP% ×2.
**Substat priority**: Energy Regen (until 260%) > Crit DMG > HP% > Crit Rate > Basic DMG% > HP.

### Endgame Stat Targets (Lv.90)
HP 35000-40000+ · DEF 1100+ · ATK 1100-1200+ · Crit Rate 20%+ · Crit DMG 250%+ · Energy Regen 260% (the amount needed for max buff potential) · Glacio DMG Bonus 0%+.
Note: even optimized, her personal damage is very low — investing in it is fully skippable; Energy Regen is by far the most important stat.

### Skill Priority
Intro Skill > Forte Circuit > Resonance Skill > Resonance Liberation > Basic Attack (anything but Intro skippable at minimal loss).

## App Data Comparison — 2026-09-09 full kit audit

Independent full-kit re-derivation (no prior dedicated audit doc existed for this file before this
pass). Cross-checked `CHARACTER_DATA`, `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE`, `RESONANCE_CHAIN_DATA`, `SKILL_MULTIPLIERS`,
`SKILL_ICONS`, `SEQUENCE_NAMES`, and `CHARACTER_ROTATIONS` fresh against this dump — all already matched
exactly. Found 2 real bugs:

1. **Dead/wrong duration on Sky Over Water's Crit Rate half**: `suisui.selfbuff.sky-over-water-critrate`
   (Inherent Skill Sky Over Water: "grant that hit +80% Crit Rate... once per 25s" on Intro/Awakening
   Spring) was modeled with `timing:{duration:999}` — a fake "sentinel" comment claimed this represented
   an unenforced 25s gate, but `buildBlockWindows` treats ANY non-null `timing.duration` as a real,
   time-integrated buff window — so this was actually a near-PERMANENT +80% Crit Rate spanning almost
   the whole rotation, not the single-hit bonus the kit text describes. Its own sibling block for the
   exact same real-world event, `suisui.selfbuff.sky-over-water-elemdmg` (+240% Glacio DMG on that same
   hit), already correctly modeled this as an instant, no-duration cast-scoped effect — the crit-rate
   half used a different, inconsistent, and wrong shape for one half of a single kit mechanic. Confirmed
   via direct measurement with synthetic stats (ATK 1000, HP 40000): the rotation's non-Intro damage
   total was inflated by ~28% (5855.76 real vs. 8140.93 with the bug) — Basic/Skill/Liberation hits were
   all getting crit-boosted by a bonus meant only for the Intro's own hit. Fixed by removing the duration
   to match elemDmg's own correct instant-cast scoping.
2. **`dmgFocus` wrongly included 'Outro' for a genuine 0% Outro damage share**: every other character
   carrying this tag (Calcharo/Encore/Lingyang/Chixia/Qingxiao) has a real `outroDmg`-categorized direct-
   damage hit on their own Outro. Suisui's Outro (Rippling Waters) deals ZERO personal damage per its
   own kit text — it's entirely a team ATK/All DMG Amp buff and a stance-transition trigger
   (`suisui.outro.rippling-waters` is correctly `kind:'buff'`, never `kind:'damage'`, in
   `suisui.blocks.js`). Fixed by dropping `'Outro'` from `dmgFocus` — this tag was functionally inert
   either way ('Outro' has no entry in `calcEngine.js`'s `TYPE_FOCUS_MAP`/`DMG_FOCUS_ROLE_TAG`), but the
   correction matches the established "genuine share only" standard used everywhere else in this table.

Verified via direct before/after measurement: the real `phase3-parityGolden.test.js` calc's
`legacyRawDps`/`engineDps` dropped from 1913 to 1802/1802, and the stat-panel `avgCrit` dropped from
1.445 to 1.125 (`score` 10896 -> 8483) — both real, expected consequences of correctly confining an
"on that hit only" bonus to only that hit. Both golden fixtures updated with a cited reason logged in
`phase3-parityGolden.test.js`'s own header-comment log. 1 new test added. Full suite green (1888/1888).
