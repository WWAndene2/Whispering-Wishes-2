## Review

**Ratings**: Hybrid — T4 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: shields/DMG reduction persist after switching; powerful Resonance Skill Amp Outro; an innate,
low-timing-requirement parry that generates Concerto Energy and reduces big-hit damage.

**Cons**: one of the longest support rotations in the game; her Sequences give little value to the rest
of the team unlike most supports; some of the slowest attack animations of any character, prone to
interruption; no meta place beyond contributing to big Jinhsi/Carlotta nukes, a niche not viable.

**Summary**: a 4★ Broadblade Havoc support acting as a Skill DMG Amplifier and Shielder — her Outro
(38% Resonance Skill DMG Amp) is massive for Jinhsi/Carlotta, both scaling almost entirely off Skill
DMG. But her Forte Circuit (Timed Counters) requires shields pre-generated and either getting hit or
casting Heavy Attack/Intro first, making it unreliable — Hologram content (one-shot mechanics) rules it
out entirely, and manual dodging generates Concerto more consistently than her awkward Strategic
Parries. Her Ultimate deals mediocre DEF-scaling damage but is necessary for Concerto generation. Her
Skill (self-heal + 3 consumable 15% team DMG reduction shields) is the least-flawed part of her kit.
Long Basic Attack wind-ups leave her vulnerable to interruption, complicating her already-difficult
Concerto rotation and making her prized Outro hard to access. Bottom tier overall, but holds a genuine
niche buffing Jinhsi/Carlotta nukes — directly outclassed by Zhezhi (higher damage, shorter/more
consistent rotation, Coordinated Attacks) but fairly comparable to Lumi (same 38% Outro, more cohesive
kit but even longer rotation — Taoqi's 14s Outro duration vs Lumi's 10s partially offsets this). Not
unusable, just heavily outclassed — fine to play if you like her or want big Jinhsi/Carlotta numbers.

## Build

**Best Weapons**: Discord (R5) 100.00% — listed first with explicit QOL framing: "close-to necessary...
by far and ahead the biggest contribution to your team DPS" via shortening her notoriously long
rotation. Dauntless Evernight (R5) 107.54% — higher raw %, a free exploration-reward option boosting
DEF% (the bulk of her damage) with no rotational advantage. Both are 4★ weapons (R5 max refinement).

**Best Echo Sets**: #1 Rejuvenating Glow, main echo Fallacy of No Return (strong Energy/permanent
buffing option) — Bell-Borne Geochelone is a worse but acceptable fallback if no good Fallacy is
available. Special/situational: Moonlit Clouds, main echo Impermanence Heron.

**Substats priority**: Energy Regen (until satisfied) > Crit Rate = Crit DMG > DEF% > DEF.

**Endgame stat targets (Lv.90)**: HP 15000+, DEF 1150+, ATK 1800-2200+ (personal damage investment is
fully skippable), Crit Rate 60-80%+, Crit DMG 210-260%+, Energy Regen 100-126%+ (no ER needed on
Moonlit Clouds at S5; before S5, ~126% estimated in a Carlotta+Shorekeeper team), Havoc DMG Bonus
30-60%+.

**Skill priority**: Support build — Forte Circuit > Resonance Skill > Resonance Liberation > Basic
Attack > Intro Skill. DPS build — Resonance Liberation > Forte Circuit > Resonance Skill > Basic Attack
> Intro Skill.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Sample Rotation** (not fixed — benefits from bonus Concerto wherever possible; skip pre-rotation
basics with a Concerto weapon like Discord): Basic P1-3 (pre-rotation) → Intro → Timed Counter 1-3 →
Liberation → Basic P1-4 → Skill → Outro. Echo timing: on Moonlit Clouds/Impermanence Heron, use at the
end of the rotation before Outro with a swap cancel; on Rejuvenating Glow/Bell-Borne/Fallacy, use as
needed but keep it active during the Main DPS's damage window.

**Synergies**: Carlotta (by far her best DPS to support — near-all Resonance Skill DMG, amplified 38%
by Outro; not Carlotta's best team since Taoqi's buffs take too long, but viable for nuke teams).
Jinhsi (good nuke damage via Skill, best paired with Verina since Jinhsi's Forte needs Coordinated
Attacks Taoqi doesn't provide — not meta, but good for "damage per screenshot"). Verina/Shorekeeper
(generalist Healing Supports).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, Jinhsi Nuke Team — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this
extraction; team names are directly from the source text.
