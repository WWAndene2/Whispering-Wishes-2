## Review

- DPS Tier: T3 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA Value), T4 (WW
  Value).
- **Pros**: incredibly long, forgiving burst window allowing dodging/mistakes/swapping freely; his main
  combo mixes ground and aerial combat, sometimes letting him dodge attacks naturally mid-combo; huge
  amplification options via team building make big Ultimate-window opportunities (stacking Verina+
  Yinlin Outros right before Ultimate is very potent); great Energy/Concerto efficiency thanks to a
  high-generation main combo plus spammable Deduction/Forte Skill abilities outside Ultimate;
  interruption RESISTANCE (not immunity) during his Ultimate state (Intuition), which covers most of
  his field time; was free at release.
- **Cons**: main combo takes practice to execute smoothly under pressure; all main-combo animations are
  long, leaving him vulnerable while casting (even though rarely actually interrupted).
- 5★ Electro Gauntlet specialist in strong Ultimate-burst combos, distinguished by an unusually long
  burst duration — forgiving for dodging, swapping, and even small execution mistakes. Gains
  Interruption Resistance while his Ultimate buff is active, greatly reducing reliance on Swap-Cancels
  (unlike Calcharo).
- As an Ultimate-centric character, he largely bypasses non-Ultimate abilities EXCEPT Deduction —
  generates lots of Concerto/Resonance Energy, Swap-Cancellable, and a short 5s cooldown. His main combo
  falls slightly short of the Concerto needed to reliably fire his Outro, so weaving in 1-2 extra
  Deduction casts elsewhere in the team rotation is advised.
- Ultimate (Cogitation Model) is the heart of his kit: a large cube projection dealing substantial
  Electro damage, generating solid Concerto, and entering the 24s Intuition state that enhances Basic/
  Skill/Dodge Counter/Heavy/Mid-air ATK plus his Forte gauge. Grants 3 Hypercube charges, the resource
  fueling Law of Reigns.
- Goal after entering Intuition: NOT to maximize enhanced-attack count within 24s, but to spend all 3
  Hypercubes on Law of Reigns as fast as possible (this maximizes DPS); consuming the 3rd charge ends
  Intuition immediately regardless of remaining duration.
- Two Forte gauges: standard "Capacity" (active outside Ultimate, fills via Deduction/Basic Attacks,
  swaps Deduction for the stronger Decipher at 100 — mostly bonus damage, largely irrelevant to his game
  plan, still worth Swap-Cancelling fast) and Ultimate-enhanced "Performance Capacity" (5-point charge
  system active only during Intuition, built by landing enhanced-ability hits, spent on Law of Reigns).
- Fastest Performance-Capacity-to-Law-of-Reigns combo: Divergence (2 PC, jump attack) → Mid-air Revamp
  (3 PC) → Law of Reigns (consumes 5). Since Divergence has a 7s cooldown, weave in the enhanced 3-part
  Pivot-Impale combo (1+2+2 PC) → Law of Reigns as the alternate bridge for the 2nd/3rd cube. Dodge
  Counters and character Swaps performed mid-combo also generate extra Performance Capacity (Ultimate-
  enhanced Dodge Counter grants 2), and can be used to skip ahead in the building sequence.
- Inherent Skills: Knowing stacks a 5%/8s Electro DMG Bonus (×4, 20% cap) on every Resonance Skill cast
  — since he casts Skills constantly (Deduction/Decipher/Divergence/Law of Reigns), this stays active
  through most of his combo. Focus grants Interruption Resistance during Intuition, letting him play
  freely without relying on Swap-Cancels like Calcharo must.
- Overall: a great Electro DPS with accessibility surpassing many other Ultimate-buff-based characters,
  without sacrificing raw damage — his 1.2-patch free availability made him especially accessible.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Gameplay**: extremely easy and forgiving — his rotation centers on casting 3 enhanced Skills (Law of
Reigns) during his 24s Ultimate Stance, leaving ample time to dodge, parry, or quickswap. Requires at
least 1 cast of base Deduction beforehand for Concerto — conveniently this also satisfies the 5P Void
Thunder set's activation condition, so pressing Skill then immediately Ultimate (cancelling the small
Skill hit) right before the burst is always best practice. Stacking Void Thunder to its max 2 stacks
needs 2 separate Skill casts — in optimized rotations, weaving a Skill cast in mid-teammate-rotation
(off Deduction's 4s cooldown) lets the big Ultimate opening hit benefit from the full set bonus.
**Echo timing**: usable at any point in his lengthy rotation or even before it — easiest window is
Swap-Cancelling it right after his last Law of Reigns.

**Standard Rotation**: Intro → Skill: Deduction (Instant Cancel with Ultimate) → Ultimate → Ultimate:
Skill: Divergence → Forte: Mid-Air Attack: Revamp → Forte: Skill: Law of Reigns → Ultimate: Basic:
Pivot-Impale P1 → P2 → P3 → Forte: Skill: Law of Reigns → Ultimate: Skill: Divergence → Forte: Mid-Air
Attack: Revamp → Forte: Skill: Law of Reigns → Outro.

**Advanced Tips**: he should ideally NOT be played in quickswap if a big swap-expiring Outro buff (like
Yinlin's) is active on him. Any Ultimate attack (except his first/second Basic Attacks) can be
consecutively Swap-Cancelled — top-level quickswap play weaves him in/out repeatedly, alternating with
another good-Swap-Cancel-window character (e.g. Changli) to effectively run 2 (or even 3, not
recommended) DPS at once. His Ultimate stance lasts long enough to entirely skip Forte Basic Attacks if
kept off-field long enough for Skill cooldown to refresh repeatedly — significantly extends full-
rotation time, though. Nothing stops using his Ultimate right at fight start to weave attacks in while
teammates run their Openers (common in top speedruns, though hard to make worthwhile for DPS). His
initial Ultimate cast freezes in-game timers slightly longer than most Ultimates (~1 extra second,
similar to Augusta's 2nd Ultimate timestop) — exploitable in quickswap, most commonly to land 4 Death
Messengers with Calcharo more consistently by Swap-Cancelling a Death Messenger as his Ultimate casts.

**Synergies**: Lynae/Yinlin (easily his best partners — Lynae wins by a landslide, an almost-equivalent
buff to Yinlin's Outro plus a much stronger general buffing profile; Yinlin is also easy to use, great
off-field Coordinated Attack damage, and a 20% Electro/25% Liberation DMG Amplify Outro). Changli (NOT
recommended for casual players — extremely quickswap-dependent, underperforms badly without heavy
swap-weaving from both characters, especially vs. Yinlin; but pushed to its limits, one of the
highest-damaging duos in the game). Jianxin (good option if you lack the above — 38% Liberation DMG on
Outro, a significant boost, but awkward due to her extended field time/Energy problems — only use if
you lack better options or don't want to quickswap). Yangyang (his best non-limited-5★ option if
willing to quickswap — several windows for her to get in extra attacks, no Energy problems thanks to
her Outro). Mornye/Shorekeeper/Verina (the best generalist Supports — Mornye has special synergy with
Lynae specifically via Tune Rupture/Strain response, generally the best choice when Lynae is in use;
Shorekeeper a close 2nd via massive Crit buffs; Verina most accessible if neither is available).

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Mornye is only best here when paired WITH Lynae specifically), Expert
Team (very difficult, not recommended for casual players), Budget Team (may face Energy problems,
needs more ER investment), Budget Expert Team (not recommended for casual players, benefits strongly
from Swap Cancels) — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this extraction; team names/
conditions are directly from the source text.
