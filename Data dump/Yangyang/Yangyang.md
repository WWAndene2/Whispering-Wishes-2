## Review

**Ratings**: Hybrid — T4 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: one of the only supports that groups enemies effectively; fully quickswap-friendly via an
Outro that funnels Energy (buffs don't expire on swap, unlike other buffing Hybrids); one of the
shortest rotations in the game, comparable to top-tier Sanhua; benefits heavily from higher Sequences;
free.

**Cons**: no amplification buff on her Outro puts her at a disadvantage vs Hybrids/supports that have
one, often needing quickswap to be comparable; no real meta place, outshined by stronger buffers or
better quickswap DPS; rotations get awkward when her Ultimate is on cooldown; very reliant on late
Sequences for damage AND team buffing.

**Summary**: a free 4★ Aero Sword Hybrid built around fast Concerto generation and quickswap-friendly
buffing. Forte Circuit (3-stack cap) unlocks the mid-air burst Feather Release — accessible via Heavy
Attack, Intro, Dodge Counter, mid-air swap-in, or jump, then a Basic Attack; jumping is the main,
reliable trigger used in rotations. Stacks are built mainly via Heavy Attacks and Resonance Skill (the
fastest/most reliable routes). Her Ultimate deals solid continuous damage, groups enemies, and
generates good Concerto. Her Outro is unimpressive from a meta standpoint (funnels 20 total Resonance
Energy over 5s — a rare effect shared only with Zhezhi) but is fully quickswap-compatible (no buffs
expiring on swap, unlike every other buffing Hybrid archetype like Sanhua/Mortefi/Zhezhi), which is her
one unique meta niche. Best used in quickswap hypercarry teams (Xiangli Yao or Changli), as a budget
option to Yinlin/Zhezhi. Hard to play well — her speed takes practice, and S4/S5/S6 are critical: below
S5 she's not worth using, since she lacks her S6's 20% team ATK buff and deals insignificant personal
damage. Summary: a difficult, not-meta character whose exceptional rotation speed and full quickswap
compatibility make her a surefire budget option for quickswap-friendly Hypercarry teams.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Standard Concerto Rotation** (skips Intro deliberately — it's slow and leaves Intro open for
teammates): Basic P3 → Heavy Atk → Heavy Atk: Zephyr Song → Ultimate (cancels endlag) → Basic P1 →
Heavy Atk → Heavy Atk: Zephyr Song → Skill (cancels endlag) → Jump (cancels Skill endlag) → Mid-Air
Atk: Feather Release → Outro. Uses Endlag Cancelling extensively — chaining abilities to skip their
finishing animations after damage/Forte is already granted.

**Synergies**: Carlotta (her best option after Zhezhi without heavy quickswap — decent personal damage,
very short rotation, 20% ATK buff at S6, Energy-restoring Outro). Xiangli Yao (benefits from all her
buffs and short rotation; with quickswap, straight-up the best non-limited 5★ option — falls off
without quickswap, not recommended to casual players). Changli (the "quickswap queen" — a strong pair
purely by dealing two characters' damage at once, falls off badly without quickswap, not recommended to
casual players). Verina/Shorekeeper (generalist Healing Supports).

**Example Teams**: Best Team, Budget Expert Team (×2, both explicitly "not recommended to casual
players... falls off significantly without quickswapping") — exact roster icons weren't
machine-readable in this extraction; team names/notes are directly from the source text.
