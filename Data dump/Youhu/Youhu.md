## Review

**Ratings**: Support — T4 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T4 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: unique and very large Coordinated Attack amplification buff; good full-team healing; good
carrier of supportive Echoes and sets.

**Cons**: slower attack animations; more limited team-building synergies (buff limited to Coordinated
Attackers); very Concerto-hungry, especially on the first rotation without immediate Intro access.

**Summary**: a 4★ Glacio Gauntlets support specializing in full-team heals and Coordinated Attack
amplification (exclusive to her at release). Similar role to Baizhi, but melee, higher field time, and a
more specialized/stronger Outro. Her Outro (100% Coordinated Attack DMG Amp for 28s, persists through
swaps) is the single biggest damage amplification source for any attack type in the game — buildable
teams around it, though few Coordinated Attackers exist at release. Concerto generation hinges on her
Resonance Skill/Forte combo: Skill grants Lucky Draw (random Antique) on a 15s cooldown; Intro and Dodge
Counter also grant Lucky Draw; a full Basic combo fills Frost for Heavy Attack: Frostfall (also grants
Lucky Draw). Antique Appraisal (the enhanced next Basic Attack after drawing an Antique) generates
Auspice; 4 Auspices unlock Poetic Essence. Liberation lets her choose her Antique directly. Solid support
overall thanks to healing/Echo flexibility, with a strong but narrow niche — competent but specialized,
limited at release by the small pool of Coordinated Attack DPS partners.

## Build

**Best Weapons**: Marcato (R5) — "Best weapon for Youhu by far thanks to its massive Energy Generation
% main stat and Concerto generation... A top option at S1 or S5." Gauntlets#21D (R5) — solid Energy
Generation, ATK% after dash/dodge. Abyss Surges (R1, 5★) — smaller Energy gen, larger ATK% boost, better
healing by comparison. Celestial Spiral (R5) — decent ATK%, flat Resonance Energy every 20s.

**Best Echo Sets**: #1 Rejuvenating Glow, main echo Fallacy of No Return (or Bell-Borne Geochelone as a
fallback if no good Fallacy is available). Special/situational: Moonlit Clouds, main echo Impermanence
Heron.

**Substats priority**: Energy Regen (until satisfied) > Crit Rate = Crit DMG > ATK% > ATK > Skill DMG%.

**Endgame stat targets (Lv.90)**: HP 15000+, DEF 1050+, ATK 1300+ (personal damage investment is fully
skippable), Crit Rate 60-80%+, Crit DMG 210-260%+, Energy Regen 100%, Glacio DMG Bonus 0-60%+.

**Skill priority**: Resonance Skill > Forte Circuit > Resonance Liberation > Basic Attack > Intro
Skill.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Rotation 1** [6.5s, works with any weapon, loops indefinitely]: Intro → Antique Appraisal (instant
cancel) → Ultimate → Antique Appraisal → Basic P1-4 → Skill → Antique Appraisal → Outro.

**Rotation 2** [6.37s, Marcato-dependent, needs 2-4 starting Auspice]: Intro → Antique Appraisal
(instant cancel) → Ultimate → Antique Appraisal → Forte: Poetic Essence (optional swap cancel) → Basic
P1 → Skill → Antique Appraisal (swap) → Outro.

Against bosses, pick Mask (pushes enemies away) or Ruyi (best for chasing small enemies) from the
Liberation's Antique choice prompt. Using her Liberation as the 3rd/4th Antique gives better odds at a
strong Auspice combo.

**Synergies**: Yinlin (highest-damage Coordinated Attacker, most natural pairing for her Outro).
Zhezhi (good Coordinated Attack DMG, viable pair). Mortefi (good Coordinated Attack DMG, viable pair).
None comparable to 5★ supports, but all viable.

**Example Teams**: Best Team (Calcharo — her buffs matter more with lower-damage Main DPS where
Yinlin's own damage matters more), Zhezhi Team (Lingyang, same reasoning for Zhezhi), Mortefi Team.
