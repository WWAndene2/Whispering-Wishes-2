## Review

**Ratings**: Hybrid — T3 (Tower of Adversity), T4 (Whimpering Wastes). Value Tier List: T3 (ToA
Value), T4 (WW Value).

**Pros**: one of the strongest off-field damage outputs in the game (barring full off-field Main DPS
like Phrolova); very quick rotation, improvable via Jump Cancels; very accessible weapon options, not
Signature-reliant; super consistent, rarely gets hit; provides extra Energy to the incoming character
via Inherent Skill on Outro.

**Cons**: lower damage ceiling than modern Hybrids, more niche buffing profile — easily replaced and
outclassed outside Jinhsi (long since powercrept, directly by Luuk Herssen); low quickswap headroom
(only during Basic 3); terrible Sequence scaling like most version 1 characters; Moonlit Clouds is
awkward with Impermanence Heron being a Transform Echo, so she defaults to Empyrean Anthem for comfort.

**Summary**: a 5★ Glacio Rectifier Hybrid buffing Glacio/Resonance Skill DMG while doing meaningful
off-field Coordinated Attack damage via quick on-field combos. Best DPS partners: Carlotta (since
version 2.0, uses her full buff kit) and Jinhsi (her best partner since release, the only fast-rotating
Coordinated Attacker that amplifies Skill DMG). Lingyang is an acceptable fallback. Meta-wise, she's
nowadays powercrept — she supports powercrept DPS characters (Resonance Skill amplifiers are scarce,
her main niches are Jinhsi/Carlotta, both since overshadowed by Luuk/Hiyuki). Ease of use is her big
selling point, but lacking standout personal damage, blazing rotation speed, or high buffing potential
makes her an overshadowed buffer overall.

## Gameplay and Teams

**Standard Rotation**: Intro → Basic 1-3 → Skill: Manifestation → Heavy: Conjuration → Skill: Stroke of
Genius (Jump Cancel) → Skill: Stroke of Genius (cancel endlag via Liberation) → Liberation → Skill:
Creation's Zenith (Dash Cancel into Echo, Swap, Outro on Moonlit Clouds, or Swap and Outro on Empyrean
Anthem) → Outro. Liberation is free to cast at any point in the rotation.

**Synergies**: Carlotta (her DPS of choice since version 2.0, uses her whole buff kit). Jinhsi (best
partner since her release — only fast Coordinated Attacker rotating without needing her Ultimate, while
amplifying Skill DMG). Lingyang (acceptable fallback if mastered). The Shorekeeper/Verina (best
generalist Supports for her generalist Glacio/Skill Amplifier + Coordinated Attack role).

**Example Teams**: Best Team — Verina can equal Shorekeeper in Jinhsi teams with enough crit-fishing
(especially S2 Verina). Other situational options: Hiyuki+Chisa, or Mornye as a general Support at S1+
(or R4 Discord+ as a Verina alternative) — exact roster icons weren't machine-readable in this
extraction; team names/notes are directly from the source text.

## App Data Comparison (vs. `app/src/data/characters.js` + `zhezhi.blocks.js`)

No dump file existed before this pass — created. `bestWeapon`, `bestEchoes`, `weaponAlts`, `teams`,

**Real bugs found and fixed (2026-09-03)**:
1. The DPS tier table had `['Zhezhi', 'T2', 'T3']` — the source's Ratings section gives Hybrid T3
   (ToA) / T4 (WW), not T2/T3. Fixed to `['Zhezhi', 'T3', 'T4']`.
2. Inherent Skill **Calligrapher's Touch** (self ATK+6% for 27s on Stroke of Genius/Creation's Zenith
   cast, stacking up to 3 times, 18% max) was entirely missing from both `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE['Zhezhi']`
   and `zhezhi.blocks.js` — both moves fire multiple times in the modeled rotation, making this a
   real, relevant DPS contribution. Added to both: the flat max-stack value (18) to
   `CHAR_BUFF_TABLE.selfBuffs`, and a real per-stack stacking block (6% ×3, matching this character's
   own S3 Resonance Chain node's precedent for the same trigger set) to `zhezhi.blocks.js`, anchored to
   the Stroke of Genius cast (same anchor as the existing S6 bonus-hit block).

1 test added, full suite green (1338/1338).

**Full 9-dimension Phase A pass (2026-09-04)**:
3. `zhezhi.liberation.living-canvas` and `zhezhi.chain.s5-bonus-hit` were both categorized `coordDmg`,
   but their kit text is explicit — Living Canvas: "Coordinated Attack (Glacio DMG, **considered Basic
   Attack DMG**)"; S5: "1 extra Inklit Apparition procs at 140% of Inklit Spirit's DMG (**Basic Attack
   DMG**)". Fixed both to `basicDmg`, same "counted as X" convention already applied to Rebecca/
   Camellya/Lucilla's own coordinated-attack-shaped-but-considered-Basic-ATK moves. This is exactly
   what this source's own Damage Profile shows too: Basic 78.4% (dominant — her spirits bucket as
   Basic) vs Liberation 0% (she has no direct Liberation-press nuke; everything routes through spirit
   procs).
4. `dmgFocus`/its buffs column were both `['Coordinated ATK', ...]` — with the fix above, she has ZERO
   real `coordDmg` blocks left, so this had no basis at all. Fixed to `['Basic ATK']` (78.4%, her real
   dominant bucket) in both columns. Skill (5.4% real share) stays excluded — this project's
   established ambiguous-exclude zone (4.6-5.5%).
5. `zhezhi.intro.radiant-ruin` had no `damage.category` — fixed to skillDmg (default convention).
6. Dimension 8: S6 (extra Ivory Herald proc) and Inherent Skill Calligrapher's Touch both say "on
